Bhutan Friends Mountain Adventure

An exciting Bhutan getaway packed with breathtaking mountain landscapes, ancient monasteries, lively local markets, and memorable adventures with friends.

🗓️

Duration

6 Days / 5 Nights

💰

Ideal For

Friends & Mates

Detailed Itinerary

Day 1

Arrival in Paro – Transfer to Thimphu

  • Arrive at Paro International Airport and enjoy a scenic drive to Thimphu
  • Hotel check-in and relax amidst the Himalayan surroundings
  • Explore the local markets and handicraft shops of Thimphu
  • Enjoy an evening at a traditional Bhutanese café with friends
Day 2

Discover Thimphu

  • Enjoy the best Thimphu sightseeing tour with Buddha Dordenma experience
  • Visit Memorial Chorten and Tashichho Dzong
  • Explore Simply Bhutan Living Museum and Motithang Takin Preserve
  • Spend the evening at Clock Tower Square exploring cafés and local life
Meals: Breakfast
Tickets: Included
Day 3

Thimphu – Punakha Adventure

  • Drive through the spectacular Dochula Pass with panoramic Himalayan views
  • Visit Chimi Lhakhang during your Bhutan cultural sightseeing tour
  • Explore the magnificent Punakha Dzong beside the Pho Chhu River
  • Walk across the famous Punakha Suspension Bridge and enjoy riverside views
Meals: Breakfast
Tickets: Included
Day 4

Punakha – Paro Heritage Journey

  • Breakfast at hotel and transfer to Paro
  • Visit the historic Rinpung Dzong overlooking Paro Valley
  • Explore the National Museum of Bhutan and its cultural exhibits
  • Enjoy leisure time exploring Paro's cafés and shopping streets
Meals: Breakfast
Tickets: Included
Day 5

Tiger's Nest Adventure

  • Experience the famous Tiger's Nest Monastery hiking tour in Bhutan
  • Capture breathtaking Himalayan landscapes from scenic viewpoints
  • Visit Kyichu Lhakhang, one of Bhutan's oldest temples
  • Enjoy a farewell Bhutanese cultural dinner with traditional folk performances
Meals: Breakfast
Tickets: Included
Day 6

Departure

  • Breakfast at hotel
  • Hotel check-out
  • Private transfer to Paro International Airport
  • Departure
Meals: Breakfast
