Georgia Heritage and Leisure Tour

A relaxing journey through Georgia's historic cities, breathtaking mountain landscapes, ancient monasteries, and cultural treasures—thoughtfully designed for senior citizens seeking comfort, scenic beauty, and enriching experiences.

🗓️

Duration

10 Days / 9 Nights

💰

Price Per Person

On Request
👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

Ideal For

Senior Citizens

Detailed Itinerary

Day 1

Arrival in Tbilisi

  • Arrive at Tbilisi International Airport
  • Private transfer to hotel and check-in
  • Relax after your journey and enjoy hotel facilities
  • Gentle evening walk through Old Tbilisi
  • Overnight stay in Tbilisi
Day 2

Tbilisi Heritage Tour

  • Breakfast at hotel
  • Visit Holy Trinity Cathedral (Sameba Cathedral)
  • Explore the Bridge of Peace and Rike Park
  • Enjoy a cable car ride to Narikala Fortress for panoramic city views
  • Overnight stay in Tbilisi
Meals: Breakfast
Tickets: Included
Day 3

Leisure in Tbilisi

  • Breakfast at hotel
  • Enjoy a leisurely morning at the hotel
  • Visit nearby cafés or stroll through Old Tbilisi at your own pace
  • Free afternoon for relaxation or optional shopping
  • Overnight stay in Tbilisi
Meals: Breakfast
Day 4

Mtskheta & Jvari Monastery

  • Breakfast at hotel
  • Visit the UNESCO-listed Jvari Monastery
  • Explore Svetitskhoveli Cathedral in Mtskheta
  • Visit the impressive Chronicles of Georgia Monument
  • Return to Tbilisi for overnight stay
Meals: Breakfast
Tickets: Included
Day 5

Ananuri & Gudauri Scenic Drive

  • Breakfast at hotel
  • Drive along the scenic Georgian Military Highway
  • Stop at the beautiful Jinvali Reservoir
  • Visit the historic Ananuri Fortress and Gudauri Panorama
  • Return to Tbilisi for overnight stay
Meals: Breakfast
Tickets: Included
Day 6

Rest & Cultural Experience

  • Breakfast at hotel
  • Free morning to relax at the hotel
  • Visit a local café or cultural center in the afternoon
  • Leisure evening in Tbilisi
  • Overnight stay in Tbilisi
Meals: Breakfast
Day 7

Kakheti Countryside

  • Breakfast at hotel
  • Visit the charming hilltop town of Sighnaghi
  • Explore Bodbe Monastery and its tranquil gardens
  • Enjoy leisure time at a local café or for souvenir shopping
  • Overnight stay in Tbilisi
Meals: Breakfast
Tickets: Included
Day 8

Scenic Tbilisi

  • Breakfast at hotel
  • Visit Mtatsminda Park for scenic city views (easy access by funicular)
  • Spend time relaxing in the park and nearby cafés
  • Return to hotel for leisure
  • Overnight stay in Tbilisi
Meals: Breakfast
Day 9

Farewell Leisure Day

  • Breakfast at hotel
  • Free day to revisit favorite places or enjoy shopping for local souvenirs
  • Relax at the hotel before your departure
  • Overnight stay in Tbilisi
Meals: Breakfast
Day 10

Departure

  • Breakfast at hotel
  • Hotel check-out
  • Private transfer to Tbilisi International Airport
  • Departure with cherished memories of Georgia
  • Safe onward journey
Meals: Breakfast
