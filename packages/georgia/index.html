Best Time to Visit Georgia

🌸 Peak Season

April – June & September – October

Pleasant weather, blooming landscapes, vibrant autumn colours, and clear mountain views.

Best For: Sightseeing, hiking, wine tours, road trips & photography

🌤️ Shoulder Season

March & November

Fewer crowds, comfortable temperatures, and great value for travellers.

Best For: Cultural tours, city breaks, budget travel & local experiences

❄️ Off Season

December – February

Snow-covered mountains, festive atmosphere, and excellent ski conditions.

Best For: Skiing, snowboarding, winter holidays & cozy mountain escapes

Visa Requirements for Georgia

🛂 Indian Citizens

Entry Requirement: e-Visa Required (Visa-free entry available for eligible travelers holding a valid visa or residence permit from the USA, UK, Schengen countries, Japan, and certain other eligible countries.)

Estimated Government Processing Time: Approximately 5 working days (may vary depending on application volume)

Estimated Government Visa Fee: USD 20 government consular fee + USD 15 mandatory verification fee (DuVerify), totaling USD 35 per applicant

General Documentation Requirements: Valid passport (minimum 3 months validity beyond the visa period), recent passport-size photograph, confirmed hotel booking, return/onward flight ticket, travel & health insurance, proof of sufficient financial means, and supporting travel documents as required

📋 Application Process

Apply online or through embassy/consulate. Our visa assistance service can help with the entire process.
