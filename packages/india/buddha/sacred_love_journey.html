Sacred Love Journey

A romantic and soul-enriching journey through the sacred Buddhist destinations of India & Nepal, blending spirituality, peaceful moments, scenic landscapes, and unforgettable experiences together.

Duration

7 Days / 6 Nights

Detailed Itinerary

Day 1

Arrival in Bodh Gaya

  • Arrive at Gaya International Airport / Railway Station
  • Private transfer to hotel and check-in
  • Visit the Mahabodhi Temple Complex at sunset
  • Spend quiet moments beneath the sacred Bodhi Tree
  • Overnight stay in Bodh Gaya
Day 2

Bodh Gaya – Nalanda – Rajgir

  • Breakfast at hotel
  • Visit the UNESCO-listed Nalanda University Ruins
  • Continue to Rajgir and enjoy the Ropeway to Vishwa Shanti Stupa
  • Evening stroll through the peaceful Venu Vana Bamboo Grove
  • Overnight stay in Rajgir
Meals: Breakfast
Tickets: Included
Day 3

Rajgir – Vaishali – Kushinagar

  • Breakfast at hotel
  • Visit Ashoka Pillar and Buddha Relic Stupas at Vaishali
  • Continue to Kushinagar
  • Visit Mahaparinirvana Temple and Reclining Buddha Statue
  • Overnight stay in Kushinagar
Meals: Breakfast
Tickets: Included
Day 4

Kushinagar – Lumbini

  • Breakfast at hotel
  • Cross into Nepal and arrive in Lumbini
  • Visit Maya Devi Temple and Sacred Garden
  • Walk through the tranquil Monastic Zone together
  • Overnight stay in Lumbini
Meals: Breakfast
Tickets: Included
Day 5

Lumbini – Sarnath

  • Breakfast at hotel
  • Drive to Sarnath
  • Visit Dhamek Stupa and Mulagandha Kuti Vihara
  • Explore Sarnath Museum and beautifully landscaped gardens
  • Evening arrival in Varanasi
  • Overnight stay in Varanasi
Meals: Breakfast
Tickets: Included
Day 6

Varanasi

  • Breakfast at hotel
  • Enjoy a peaceful sunrise boat ride on the River Ganges (optional)
  • Explore the charming lanes and local markets of Varanasi
  • Witness the mesmerizing Ganga Aarti together in the evening (optional)
  • Overnight stay in Varanasi
Meals: Breakfast
Day 7

Departure

  • Breakfast at hotel
  • Check-out from hotel
  • Private transfer to Varanasi Airport / Railway Station
  • Departure with unforgettable memories together
  • Safe onward journey
Meals: Breakfast
