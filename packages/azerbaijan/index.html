Best Time to Visit Azerbaijan

🌞 Peak Season

April – June & September – October

Pleasant weather and clear skies.

Best For: Sightseeing, nature, city tours and photography

🌤️ Shoulder Season

March & November

Mild weather with fewer crowds.

Best For: Budget travel, cultural tours and relaxed holidays

❄️ Off Season

December – February & July – August

Cold winters or hot summers.

Best For: Skiing, budget trips and indoor attractions

Visa Requirements for Azerbaijan

🛂 Indian Citizens

Entry Requirement: ASAN Tourist E-Visa

Estimated Government Processing Time: Approximately 3 working days (Standard) or 3 hours (Urgent), subject to approval by the relevant authorities.

Estimated Government Visa Fee: Approximately ₹2,745 (Legitimate fee: USD 29 = USD 20 government fee + USD 9 service fee). INR amount may vary based on the exchange rate.

General Documentation Requirements: Valid passport (minimum 6 months validity), passport scan, completed online application, valid email address, and online payment.

📋 Application Process

Applications can be submitted online through the Authorized ASAN Visa portal. Trippovention provides documentation guidance and application assistance to help travelers complete the application accurately. Final approval is granted solely by the relevant government authorities.
