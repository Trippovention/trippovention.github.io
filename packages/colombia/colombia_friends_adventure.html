Day 1

Arrival in Bogotá

  • Arrival & Bogotá Nightlife & Graffiti Tour Kickoff
  • Arrive at Bogotá Airport & private transfer to hotel
  • Hotel check-in & relax after arrival
  • Explore Zona Rosa - Bogotá’s best nightlife & restaurants
  • Optional pub crawl or group dinner
Meals: As per itinerary
Day 2

Bogotá Sightseeing + Graffiti Tour

  • History Meets Street Art
  • Visit the Gold Museum
  • Explore La Candelaria historic district
  • Join the famous Bogotá Graffiti Tour
  • Free evening for shopping or craft beer tasting
Meals: Breakfast
Tickets: Included
Day 3

Zipaquirá Salt Cathedral + Fly to Medellín

  • Zipaquirá Salt Cathedral Experience & City Transfer
  • Day trip to Zipaquirá Salt Cathedral
  • Explore underground tunnels & light displays
  • Return to Bogotá & transfer to airport
  • Fly to Medellín, hotel check-in & relax
Meals: Breakfast
Tickets: Included
Day 4

Medellín City & Comuna 13 Tour

  • Medellín Comuna 13 & Party Vibes
  • Guided tour of Comuna 13 street art area
  • Ride the outdoor escalators & enjoy performances
  • Visit Pueblito Paisa for city views
  • Evening at Parque Lleras nightlife zone
Meals: Breakfast
Tickets: Included
Day 5

Guatapé + Peñol Rock Adventure

  • Guatapé & Peñol Rock Adventure
  • Full-day trip to Guatapé
  • Climb Peñol Rock for panoramic lake views
  • Explore colorful Guatapé town
  • Return to Medellín & relax
Meals: Breakfast
Tickets: Included
Day 6

Fly to Cartagena & Beach Evening

  • Caribbean Vibes Start
  • Fly from Medellín → Cartagena
  • Transfer to hotel & check-in
  • Evening at Bocagrande Beach
  • Group dinner in Getsemaní (On Your Own)
Meals: Breakfast
Day 7

Rosario Islands Party Boat

  • Cartagena & Rosario Islands Party Boat
  • Full-day party boat tour to Rosario Islands
  • Snorkeling in clear Caribbean waters
  • Beach club access with music & lounges
  • Return to Cartagena by evening
Meals: Breakfast & Lunch
Tickets: Included
Day 8

Cartagena City & Nightlife

  • Culture & Party Night
  • Walking tour of Cartagena Old Town
  • Visit San Felipe Castle
  • Free time for café hopping or shopping
  • Nightlife at salsa bars or rooftop lounges
Meals: Breakfast
Tickets: Included
Day 9

Cartagena Leisure Day

  • Chill Time With Your Group
  • Free day to relax at your own pace
  • Optional water sports or beach club visit
  • Rum tasting or cooking class experience
  • Sunset cruise or relaxed group evening
Meals: Breakfast
Day 10

Departure

  • Goodbye Colombia
  • Breakfast at hotel
  • Hotel check-out
  • Private transfer to Cartagena Airport
  • Departure with unforgettable group memories
Meals: Breakfast
