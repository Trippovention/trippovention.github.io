Visa Service Terms & Conditions

Payment policy, bank details, and operational guidelines for all Trippovention visa assistance services.

Visa service terms This page is the canonical operational terms document for all destinations (payment, refunds, bank transfer details, document handling). For general website terms, see Site Terms & Conditions.

1. Visa Decision

Trippovention provides visa consultation and application assistance based on its experience and knowledge. However, we do not guarantee the approval or refusal of any visa application. The final decision rests solely with the respective Embassy, Consulate, Visa Application Centre, or Immigration Authority.

2. Document Submission

Clients are responsible for providing complete, accurate, and genuine documents. A signed document checklist or receipt will be issued upon submission of documents to our representative. Trippovention shall not be liable for delays, refusals, or losses arising from incomplete, inaccurate, forged, or fraudulent documentation provided by the client.

3. Visa Application Processing

A minimum of one (1) working day is required to prepare and submit a visa application after receiving all the required documents. Additional processing time may be required depending on the complexity of the application or if further documentation is requested by the Embassy.

4. Pick-up & Drop-off Services

Pick-up and drop-off services for physical documents and passports are available upon request. Charges are location-based, with a minimum charge of INR 200 per location.

5. Embassy Processing Timelines

Visa processing timelines are determined solely by the respective Embassy, Consulate, or Visa Application Centre. Timelines vary by destination and visa category. Trippovention has no control over visa processing times, interview schedules, administrative processing, appointment availability, or visa decisions. Processing timelines may be extended or shortened by the Embassy without prior notice.

6. Service Charges

Trippovention provides pre-visa and post-visa assistance as agreed with the client. Once an application has been accepted for processing, all service charges, convenience fees, courier charges, discounts, and pick-up/drop-off charges are strictly non-refundable.

7. Document Handling

All original documents submitted for visa processing will be forwarded to the respective Embassy or Visa Application Centre as required. Documents will be returned only if released by the Embassy or Visa Application Centre. Passports will be released to the client only after full payment of all outstanding dues has been received.

8. Refund Policy

Refund requests will be considered only when submitted with a valid reason and supporting documentation, wherever applicable.

  • Approval of any refund request shall be at the sole discretion of Trippovention Management.
  • Trippovention reserves the right to issue a Credit Note in place of a monetary refund. Such Credit Note may be redeemed against future services offered by Trippovention.
  • Approved monetary refunds shall be processed only through Bank Transfer or Credit Card, depending on the original mode of payment and subject to approval. Cash refunds will not be entertained.
  • Applicable service charges for services already rendered are non-refundable and shall be deducted from any approved refund.
  • Approved refunds will normally be processed within 15 working days from the date of approval.

9. Amendment Policy

Any correction, amendment, or modification requested after completion of the visa application form shall attract additional service charges.

  • Changes to travel dates, itinerary, accommodation, flights, or supporting documents after processing has commenced may incur additional service charges.
  • Rescheduling or modifying a visa appointment, where permitted, shall be subject to appointment availability and applicable service charges.
  • If an amendment requires submission of a fresh visa application, the applicable visa fees and Trippovention service charges shall be payable again.

10. Payment Policy & Official Bank Details

Trippovention operates on a 100% advance payment basis. No credit facility is available.

Accepted Payment Methods: Bank Transfer, Cheque, Demand Draft, UPI, Cash (as per applicable Government regulations), Debit/Credit Card.
