Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to key questions about North American visas

Can I visit Mexico if I have a valid US visa?

Yes, Indian passport holders with a valid, multiple-entry visa for the United States, Canada, Japan, the United Kingdom, or any Schengen state are exempt from Mexican tourist visa requirements for stays under 180 days.

How long is a Canada Visitor Visa valid for?

A Canadian multiple-entry visitor visa is typically granted for up to 10 years or until one month before your passport expires, allowing stays of up to 6 months per entry.

What does Trippovention's USA B1/B2 visa assistance include?

We provide end-to-end guidance including DS-160 application review, consular MRV fee payment, appointment slot tracking (OFC & consular), customized interview preparation, and mock interview consultation.
