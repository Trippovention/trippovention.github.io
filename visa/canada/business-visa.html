Canada Business Visitor Visa Document Checklist

Complete document requirements for corporate delegates & business travelers

Mandatory Basic Documents

  • Appointment Confirmation Letter: Official appointment confirmation letter.
  • Completed Application Form: Completed online visa application form.
  • Personal Covering Letter: Stating travel purpose, business details, and itinerary.
  • Flight & Hotel Bookings: Confirmed round-trip flight itinerary and hotel reservation in Canada.
  • Original Passport: Minimum 6 months validity from travel date; clear colour scans of all used pages including bio page.
  • Previous Passports: Colour scans of all used pages of old passport(s).
  • Recent Photograph: 1 recent HD passport-size photo soft copy (white background, face & eyes direct to camera, taken within 3 months).
  • Aadhaar Card: Clear scan of applicant's Aadhaar card.

Financial Documents

  • Personal Bank Statement: 6 months personal bank statement (stamped by bank).
  • Proof of Financial Assets: Fixed Deposits (FD), Property Papers, Mutual Funds, or investment papers (if available).

Business Documents (Indian Employer / Business)

  • Business Covering Letter: Printed on official corporate letterhead with company stamp and authorized signature.
  • Company Registration: Certificate of incorporation, Memorandum of Association, or Partnership deed.
  • GST Certificate: Company GST registration certificate.
  • Business Card: Applicant's corporate business card.
  • Company Bank Statements: 6 months corporate bank account statements (stamped by bank).
  • Company ITR: Corporate Income Tax Returns for the last 3 years (if applicable).

Canadian Inviting Company Documents

  • Invitation Letter: Official invitation letter on Canadian company letterhead outlining meeting details, dates, and purpose.
  • Business Registration: Canadian company registration or business license copy (if available).
  • Signatory ID: Canadian host signatory passport / ID copy.
  • Inviter Business Card: Canadian host business card.
  • Company Contact Details: Full address, official email, and phone contact of Canadian inviting company.

If Sponsored Trip

  • Sponsorship Letter: Official letter confirming sponsorship of travel, accommodation, and daily expenses.
  • Sponsor ID: Passport / Registration document of sponsor entity.
  • Sponsor Bank Statement: 6 months bank statement of sponsor (stamped by bank).
  • Sponsor Tax Documents: Sponsor Income Tax Returns / audited financials.

Additional Business Proofs

  • Meeting Schedule: Detailed agenda / schedule of business meetings in Canada.
  • Conference Registration: Registration confirmation ticket / badge (if attending expo or trade show).
  • Business Correspondence: Official email exchanges between Indian and Canadian entities.
  • Contracts & Purchase Orders: Trade agreements, MOUs, or purchase orders (if available).
  • Travel History Proof: Proof of previous international business travel (stamps / visas).

Important Application Notes

  • Submit clear, high-resolution colour scans of all documents.
  • Official certified English or French translations are mandatory for any non-English/French documents.
  • IRCC (Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada) may request additional documents during application review.
  • Visa approval is strictly at IRCC's discretion.

Frequently Asked Questions — Canada Business Visa

Quick answers to key questions about Canadian host invitation, requirements, and fees

What documents are required from the Canadian inviting company?

The Canadian host company must issue an official Invitation Letter on letterhead outlining the purpose of visit, meeting dates, signatory ID copy, host business card, business registration copy, and company contact details.

Can I conduct long-term work on a Canada Business Visitor Visa?

No, a Business Visitor Visa is meant strictly for short-term business activities such as meetings, trade conferences, site visits, and consultations without directly entering the Canadian labor market.

What is the fee for Canada Business Visa assistance?

The total fee is ₹ 22,900 per applicant (₹ 12,900 CAD 185 Embassy Fee + ₹ 10,000 Trippovention Consultation Charges).

How long does Canada Business Visa processing take?

Processing typically takes 25 to 40 working days after document filing and biometrics submission.
