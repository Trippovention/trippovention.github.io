Document Checklist

Complete requirements for leisure holidays & sightseeing in Canada

Mandatory Basic Documents

  • Appointment Confirmation Letter: Official appointment confirmation letter.
  • Completed Application Form: Completed online visa application form.
  • Personal Covering Letter: Explaining purpose of travel & detailed itinerary (family applications: include copy with each application).
  • Flight & Hotel Bookings: Confirmed round-trip flight itinerary and hotel reservations.
  • Valid Passport: Minimum 6 months validity from travel date; clear colour scans of all used pages including bio page.
  • Passport Photo: 1 recent HD passport-size photo soft copy (white background, face & eyes direct to camera, taken within 3 months).
  • Previous Passports: Colour scans of all used pages of old passport(s).
  • Aadhaar Card: Clear scan of applicant's Aadhaar card.

For Self-Employed Applicants

  • Covering Letter: Printed on official company letterhead with stamp and signature.
  • Business Card: Applicant's corporate business card.
  • Bank Statements: 6 months personal and company bank statements (stamped by bank).
  • ITR: Income Tax Returns for the last 3 years.
  • Business Registration: GST registration certificate or company incorporation proof.

For Salaried Applicants

  • Bank Statement: Salary account bank statement for the last 6 months (stamped by bank).
  • Employer NOC: No Objection Certificate from employer approving leave dates.
  • Salary Slips: Official salary slips for the last 6 months.
  • ITR: Personal Income Tax Returns for the last 3 years.

For Retired Applicants

  • Retirement Proof: Retirement certificate / pension approval letter.
  • Pension Statements: Pension statements (last 6 months).
  • Other Income Proof: Other Income Proof (if available).

If Sponsored

  • Sponsor Identification: Sponsor Passport / PAN / Resident Permit copy.
  • Sponsor Bank Statement: 6 months bank statement of sponsor (stamped by bank).
  • Sponsor Financials: 6 months salary slips & 3 years ITR of sponsor.

If Student

  • Student ID: Valid school / college / university student ID card & leave approval letter.

Important Application Notes

  • All documents submitted must be clear, high-resolution colour scans.
  • Additional supporting documents may be requested by immigration authorities during processing.
  • Visa approval is at the sole discretion of the High Commission / Government of Canada.

Frequently Asked Questions — Canada Tourist Visa

Quick answers to key questions about processing, fees, and requirements

What is a Canada Tourist Visa?

A Canada Tourist Visa (Temporary Resident Visa / TRV) allows eligible Indian travellers to visit Canada for tourism, sightseeing, holidays, and leisure travel.

How long can I stay in Canada on a Tourist Visa?

Most visitors are generally allowed to stay for up to 6 months per visit, subject to the entry stamp given by border services officers.

What primary documents are required for a Canada Tourist Visa?

Primary requirements include valid passport (all used pages + bio page), IRCC portal application forms, personal cover letter, 6 months bank statement attested by bank, ITR for last 2-3 years, employment/business proofs, flight & hotel vouchers.

Is biometrics enrolment mandatory for a Canada Tourist Visa?

Yes, applicants aged 14 to 79 years must provide biometrics (fingerprints and digital photo) at a VFS Global Visa Application Centre (VAC) after submitting their online application.

How long does Canada Tourist Visa processing take?

Processing typically takes 25 to 40 working days after IRCC online filing and biometrics enrolment.

What minimum bank balance is recommended for a Canada Tourist Visa?

Applicants should present a healthy 6-month bank statement with sufficient funds (ideally INR 3,00,000 to 5,00,000+ per applicant) to demonstrate financial capacity and strong home ties.
