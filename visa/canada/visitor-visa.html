Document Checklist

Complete requirements for visiting family, friends, or host sponsors in Canada

Mandatory Basic Documents

  • Appointment Confirmation Letter: Official appointment confirmation letter.
  • Completed Application Form: Completed online visa application form.
  • Personal Covering Letter: Explaining purpose of travel & detailed itinerary (family applications: include copy with each application).
  • Flight & Hotel Bookings: Confirmed round-trip flight itinerary and hotel / host accommodation details.
  • Valid Passport: Minimum 6 months validity from travel date; clear colour scans of all used pages including bio page.
  • Passport Photo: 1 recent HD passport-size photo soft copy (white background, face & eyes direct to camera, taken within 3 months).
  • Previous Passports: Colour scans of all used pages of old passport(s).
  • Aadhaar Card: Clear scan of applicant's Aadhaar card.

For Self-Employed Applicants

  • Covering Letter: Printed on official company letterhead with stamp and signature.
  • Business Card: Applicant's corporate business card.
  • Bank Statements: 6 months personal and company bank statements (stamped by bank).
  • ITR: Income Tax Returns for the last 3 years.
  • Business Registration: GST registration certificate or company incorporation proof.

For Salaried Applicants

  • Bank Statement: Salary account bank statement for the last 6 months (stamped by bank).
  • Employer NOC: No Objection Certificate from employer approving leave dates.
  • Salary Slips: Official salary slips for the last 6 months.
  • ITR: Personal Income Tax Returns for the last 3 years.

If Visiting Family / Friends in Canada

  • Invitation Letter: Signed invitation letter from host detailing relationship, stay arrangements, and purpose of visit.
  • Invitee Passport Copy: Copy of host's passport bio page.
  • Canada Status Proof: Host's PR Card / Work Permit / Study Permit / Canadian Citizenship proof.
  • Invitee Financials: Host's 6 months bank statement in Canada.
  • Host Address Proof: Tenancy agreement, lease, or utility bill in Canada.
  • Occupation Proof: Host's job letter or employment proof (if available).

If Sponsored

  • Sponsor Identification: Sponsor Passport / PAN / Resident Permit copy.
  • Sponsor Bank Statement: 6 months bank statement of sponsor (stamped by bank).
  • Sponsor Financials: 6 months salary slips & 3 years ITR of sponsor.

If Student

  • Student ID: Valid school / college / university student ID card & leave approval letter.

Important Application Notes

  • All documents submitted must be clear, high-resolution colour scans.
  • Additional supporting documents may be requested by immigration authorities during processing.
  • Visa approval is at the sole discretion of the High Commission / Government of Canada.

Frequently Asked Questions — Canada Visitor Visa

Quick answers to key questions about host requirements, processing, and fees

What documents are required from my host in Canada?

Your host in Canada needs to provide a signed Invitation Letter, passport bio page, proof of status (PR card, study/work permit, or citizenship certificate), 6-month bank statement (if sponsoring), Notice of Assessment (NOA), and address proof (lease or utility bill).

How long can I stay in Canada on a Visitor Visa?

Most visitors are granted a stay of up to 6 months per entry by border services officers upon arrival into Canada.

Can a family member or relative sponsor my Canada Visitor Visa?

Yes, a relative in Canada can sponsor your trip by providing an invitation letter, proof of Canadian status, NOA/tax returns, employment letter, and bank statements showing adequate funds to support your stay.

How long does Canada Visitor Visa processing take?

Processing typically takes 25 to 40 working days after IRCC online application filing and biometrics enrolment.

Is biometrics enrolment mandatory for a Canada Visitor Visa?

Yes, first-time applicants aged 14 to 79 must provide biometrics (fingerprints and digital photo) at a VFS Global Visa Application Centre (VAC) after submitting their online application.

What is the validity of a Canada Visitor Visa?

Canada Visitor Visas issued to Indian passport holders are typically multiple-entry visas valid for up to 10 years (or until passport expiry), allowing stays of up to 6 months per visit.

What minimum bank balance should the applicant and host show for a Canada Visitor Visa?

The applicant in India should present a 6-month bank statement with an active balance of at least INR 3,00,000 to 5,00,000+. If the host in Canada is covering travel expenses, they should also provide their Canadian bank statement and proof of income (NOA/T4).

What proof of relationship is required when applying for a Canada Visitor Visa?

Depending on the relationship, supporting proof includes birth certificates (for parents/children), marriage certificates (for spouses), family tree documentation, or joint photos and past travel/communication records.
