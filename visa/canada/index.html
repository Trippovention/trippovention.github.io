Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to key questions about processing, fees, and requirements

How long does Canada Visa processing take?

Canada Visa processing typically takes 25 to 40 working days after IRCC online application filing and biometrics enrolment.

What primary documents are required for Canada Visa?

Primary requirements include valid passport (all used pages + bio page), IRCC portal application forms, personal cover letter, 6 months bank statement attested by bank, ITR for last 2-3 years, employment/business proofs, flight & hotel vouchers, and invitation letter (if applicable).

Is biometrics enrolment mandatory for Canada Visa applicants from India?

Yes, first-time applicants aged 14 to 79 must provide biometrics (fingerprints and photograph) at a VFS Global Visa Application Centre (VAC) after submitting their online application.

What is the validity of a Canada Visitor / Tourist Visa?

Canada Visitor Visas issued to Indian passport holders are typically multiple-entry visas valid for up to 10 years (or until passport expiry), allowing stays of up to 6 months per visit.

What minimum bank balance is recommended for Canada Visa?

Applicants should provide a healthy, stable 6-month bank statement with sufficient funds (ideally INR 3,00,000 to 5,00,000+ per applicant) to demonstrate financial capacity and strong ties to India.

Does Trippovention provide end-to-end Canada Visa filing assistance?

Yes, Trippovention provides complete document verification, SOP & Cover Letter drafting, IRCC portal file submission, VFS biometrics appointment scheduling, and application status tracking.
