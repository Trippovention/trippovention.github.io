Document Checklist

Complete requirements for Indian citizens applying for United Kingdom Business Visa

Primary Documents

  • Completed Visa Application Form: Completed and signed online UK visa application form.
  • Personal Covering Letter: Original letter with travel plan. If the applicant is submitting the applications of his/her family, then all applications must include a copy of the covering letter.
  • Flight Reservation: Confirmed round-trip flight booking or travel itinerary.
  • Proof of Accommodation: Hotel booking confirmation or UK stay arrangements proof.
  • Travel Insurance: Minimum coverage of 30,000 Euro / 50,000 USD / 50,000 CHF per person. Coverage of all expenses which might arise in connection with repatriation for medical reasons, urgent medical attention and/or emergency hospital treatment for the entire stay.
  • Valid National Passport: Issued within the last 10 years and with at least 6 months validity after the scheduled return, with at least two blank pages. (Handwritten passports and passports with observations regarding the front bio page cannot be accepted).
  • Photocopy of Passport: Clear photocopy of the passport (Front & Back bio-data pages).
  • Passport Size Pictures: A recent passport-size colour photograph with white background not older than 3 months, showing your head and shoulders, with your face covering 70–80% of the image (size 35–40 mm in width, not copied or scanned picture). Digital soft copy is required.
  • Previous Passports: If any, bound together with rubber band, not stapled. Photocopy of previous UK, USA, and Schengen visa copies.

Financial Documents

  • Proof of Financial Status: All documents have to be originals, in format A4, with stamp and signature of Bank.
  • Personal ITR: Personal Income Tax Returns (ITR) of the last 3 years.

Category-Specific Additional Requirements

For Self-Employed

  • Covering Letter: Covering letter has to be printed on the company letterhead.
  • Business Card: Corporate visiting / business card.
  • Company Bank Statement: Company bank statement of last 6 months with stamp & signature from bank.
  • Company Registration Proof: Business registration proof or Certificate of Incorporation / GST.

For Employed

  • NOC from Employer: NOC from the company on the official company letterhead.
  • Recognition Letter: Recognition letter from the company.
  • Salary Slips: Salary slips of last six (6) months.

If Sponsored

  • Sponsorship Letter: Sponsorship letter from the company on the official company letterhead.
  • Bank Statement: Updated bank statement of last 6 months (if available).
  • Tax Documents: Tax documents of last 3 years of the company.

Invitee Documents (UK Host Company)

  • Company Registration Proof: UK invitee company registration proof.
  • Invitation Letter: Official Invitation Letter on the company’s letterhead.
  • Passport Copy / Status Proof: Passport copy of the authorized person from the invitee company (Front & Back) or UK legal status proof.
  • Bank Statement: Bank statement of invitee for last 6 months (if available).
  • Tax Documents: Tax documents of last 3 years of the company (if available).
