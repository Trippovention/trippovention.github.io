Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to key questions about processing, appointments, requirements, and validity

How long does United Kingdom Visa processing take for Indian citizens?

Standard United Kingdom Visa processing typically takes 15 to 21 working days (approx. 3 weeks) after submitting your biometrics and passport at the VFS Global application centre. Priority processing (5 working days) and Super Priority (next working day) options may also be available subject to VFS slot availability.

What are the primary documents required for a UK Visa application?

Key requirements include a valid original passport (with at least 6 months validity post-return and 2 blank pages), completed online application form, personal covering letter with daily travel itinerary, confirmed flight bookings and hotel vouchers, travel medical insurance, last 6 months original bank statement in A4 format (stamped & signed), and personal ITR for the last 3 years.

What validity options are available for a UK Standard Visitor Visa?

UK Standard Visitor Visas are available in four validity tiers: Short-term (up to 6 months), or longer-term multi-entry visas for 2 years, 5 years, or 10 years. In most instances, each individual visit allows a maximum stay of up to 6 months.

Is a biometrics appointment mandatory for a UK Visa in India?

Yes, all applicants (including children and infants) must attend an in-person biometric appointment at an authorized VFS Global centre in India to provide digital fingerprints, take a digital facial photo, and submit their passport.

What are the specific bank statement requirements for a UK Visa?

UKVI requires original bank statements covering the last 6 months, strictly in A4 format, bearing the official stamp and signature of the issuing bank. The statements must show consistent transactions, regular monthly income, and sufficient funds without large unexplained lump-sum deposits.

Can I travel to European Schengen countries with a UK Visa?

No. The United Kingdom is not a Schengen member. A UK Standard Visitor Visa permits travel across England, Scotland, Wales, and Northern Ireland. If your itinerary includes European countries like France, Switzerland, or Italy, you must apply for a separate Schengen Visa.

What is the UK eVisa system and how does it work?

The UK Home Office is transitioning visa holders to digital eVisas, replacing physical passport stickers and BRP cards with an online digital immigration record. Trippovention provides complete guidance for UK eVisa account creation and linking.

Can I reapply if my UK Visa application was previously refused?

Yes. Standard visitor visa refusals do not carry a right of appeal, but you are entitled to submit a fresh application. Our visa team conducts an in-depth audit of your refusal letter, remedies all noted deficiencies, and prepares a reinforced re-application package.
