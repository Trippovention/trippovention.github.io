Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to key questions about South American visas

Do Indians need a visa for Peru?

Indian nationals holding a valid multiple-entry visa with minimum 6 months validity for the USA, Canada, UK, Australia, or Schengen area do not require a tourist visa to enter Peru for stays up to 180 days.

What is the Argentina AVE Electronic Travel Authorization?

Indian citizens holding a valid US B2 or Schengen Category C visa can apply for the Argentine AVE online rather than submitting a physical application at the consulate.

What is required to apply for a Brazil tourist visa (VIVIS)?

Indian citizens need a completed online RVD protocol form, passport with 6 months validity, passport photos meeting consulate specs, return flight reservation, proof of accommodation, and 3 to 6 months bank statements with adequate funds.
