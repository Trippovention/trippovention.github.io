Document Checklist

Complete physical document requirements for corporate delegates & business travelers

Primary Documents

  • Completed Visa Application Form: Completely filled and signed by the applicant.
  • Personal Covering Letter: Original letter detailing business purpose and travel plan. If the applicant is submitting applications for colleagues or family, all applications must include a copy of the covering letter.
  • Flight Reservation: Confirmed round-trip flight booking with passenger names and intra-Schengen travel itinerary.
  • Proof of Accommodation: Confirmed hotel booking or accommodation voucher covering the entire stay in Austria and the Schengen territory.
  • Travel Insurance: Minimum coverage of 30,000 Euro / 50,000 USD / 50,000 CHF per person. Coverage of all expenses which might arise in connection with repatriation for medical reasons, urgent medical attention, and/or emergency hospital treatment across the entire Schengen territory.
  • Valid National Passport: Issued within the last 10 years, with at least 6 months validity after scheduled return date, and at least two blank pages. Handwritten passports and passports with observations on the front bio page cannot be accepted.
  • Photocopy of Passport: Clear photocopy of the passport bio pages (front & back).
  • Passport Size Pictures: Recent passport-size color photograph with white background not older than 3 months, showing head and shoulders with face covering 70–80% of the image (size 35–40 mm in width, not copied or scanned). Soft copy is required.
  • Previous Passport/s: If any, bound together with rubber band (not stapled), along with photocopies of previous UK, USA, and Schengen visa copies.

Financial Documents

  • Personal Bank Statement: Original personal bank statement of the last 6 months with official bank stamp and signature (strictly original format A4 paper).
  • Income Tax Returns: Personal ITR or tax documents for the last 3 years.

Category-Specific Additional Requirements

For Self-Employed:

  • Covering letter printed on official company letterhead.
  • Business / Visiting card.
  • Company bank statement of the last 6 months with stamp & signature from bank (format A4).
  • Business Registration Proof (GST certificate, Certificate of Incorporation, or Partnership deed).

For Employed:

  • NOC from the employer on official company letterhead.
  • Recognition letter from the employer detailing business assignment.
  • Salary slips for the last 6 months.

If Sponsored by Company:

  • Sponsorship letter from the sponsoring company on official letterhead.
  • Updated company bank statement of the last 6 months (if available).
  • Tax documents / ITR of the last 3 years of the sponsoring company.

Invitee Documents (Austrian Host Company)

  • Company Registration Proof: Official registration proof of the host company in Austria.
  • Invitation Letter: Formal business invitation letter on the Austrian company’s official letterhead stating purpose, dates, and meeting agenda.
  • Passport Copy / Status Proof: Passport copy of the authorized signatory from the invitee company (front and back) or Austrian residence permit proof.
  • Bank Statement: Bank statement of the host company for the last 6 months (if available / sponsored).
  • Tax Documents: Tax documents / financial filings of the last 3 years of the host company.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to key questions about processing, fees, and requirements

How long does Austria Business Visa processing take?

Austria Business Visa processing typically takes 10 to 15 working days upon VFS document deposit after application submission or consulate interview.

What is the fee for Austria Business Visa assistance?

The total fee for an adult is ₹ 17,524 (comprising ₹ 10,000 Embassy visa fee, ₹ 2,524 mandatory VFS centre appointment charge, and ₹ 5,000 Trippovention file preparation service charge). For children aged 6–12 years, the total fee is ₹ 12,524.

What primary documents are needed for Austria Business Visa application?

Primary requirements include a valid national passport (6+ months validity post return, 2 blank pages), signed application form, personal covering letter, flight & hotel confirmations, Schengen travel insurance (€30k coverage), official host invitation on Austrian company letterhead, recent 35–40 mm photographs, and original 6-month bank statements stamped & signed on A4 paper with 3 years ITR.

Can I get doorstep document pickup for Austria visa?

Yes, Trippovention offers physical document pick-up and delivery services across location boundaries (starting from INR 200 based on area).
