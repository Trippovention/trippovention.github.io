Document Checklist

Complete physical document requirements for VFS submission

Primary Documents

  • Completed Visa Application Form: Completely filled and signed by the applicant.
  • Personal Covering Letter: Original letter with detailed travel plan. If the applicant is submitting applications for family members, all applications must include a copy of the covering letter.
  • Flight Reservation: Confirmed round-trip flight booking with passenger names and intra-Schengen travel proof.
  • Proof of Accommodation: Confirmed hotel booking or accommodation voucher covering the entire stay across Austria and the Schengen territory.
  • Travel Insurance: Minimum coverage of 30,000 Euro / 50,000 USD / 50,000 CHF per person. Coverage of all expenses which might arise in connection with repatriation for medical reasons, urgent medical attention, and/or emergency hospital treatment across the entire Schengen territory.
  • Valid National Passport: Issued within the last 10 years and with at least 6 months validity after the scheduled return date, with at least two blank pages. Handwritten passports and passports with observations regarding the front bio page cannot be accepted.
  • Photocopy of Passport: Clear photocopy of the passport bio pages (front & back).
  • Passport Size Pictures: Recent passport-size color photograph with white background not older than 3 months, showing head and shoulders with face covering 70–80% of the image (size 35–40 mm in width, not copied or scanned). Soft copy is required.
  • Previous Passport/s: If any, bound together with rubber band (not stapled), along with photocopies of previous UK, USA, and Schengen visa copies.

Financial Documents

  • Personal Bank Statement: Original personal bank statement of the last 6 months with official bank stamp and signature from the issuing branch (strictly original format A4 paper).
  • Income Tax Returns: Personal ITR or tax documents for the last 3 years.

Category-Specific Additional Requirements

For Self-Employed:

  • Covering letter printed on official company letterhead.
  • Business / Visiting card.
  • Personal & Company bank statements of the last 6 months with stamp & signature from bank.
  • Business Registration Proof (GST certificate, Certificate of Incorporation, or Partnership deed).

For Employed:

  • NOC from the company on official company letterhead.
  • Salary slips for the last 6 months.
  • Salary account bank statement for the last 6 months (stamped & signed).

For Retired:

  • Retirement Certificate: Retirement certificate / pension approval letter.
  • Pension Statements: Pension bank statements for the last 6 months.
  • Other Income Proof: Other income proof (if available).

If Student:

  • Student ID card and bonafide school/college certificate.

If Sponsored:

  • Sponsor's national ID proof (Passport, PAN card, or Resident permit).
  • Updated bank statement of sponsor for the last 6 months (stamped & signed).
  • Salary slips of sponsor for the last 6 months.
  • Personal ITR of sponsor for the last 3 years.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to key questions about processing, fees, and requirements

How long does Austria Tourist Visa processing take?

Austria Tourist Visa processing typically takes 10 to 15 working days upon VFS document deposit after application submission or consulate interview.

What is the fee for Austria Tourist Visa assistance?

The total fee for an adult is ₹ 17,524 (comprising ₹ 10,000 Embassy visa fee, ₹ 2,524 mandatory VFS centre appointment charge, and ₹ 5,000 Trippovention file preparation service charge). For children aged 6–12 years, the total fee is ₹ 12,524.

What primary documents are needed for Austria Tourist Visa application?

Primary requirements include a valid national passport (6+ months validity post return, 2 blank pages), signed application form, personal covering letter with travel itinerary, flight & hotel confirmations, Schengen travel medical insurance (min €30,000 coverage), recent 35–40 mm photographs (70–80% face coverage), and original 6-month bank statement stamped & signed on format A4 paper with 3 years personal ITR.

Can I get doorstep document pickup for Austria visa?

Yes, Trippovention offers physical document pick-up and delivery services across location boundaries (starting from INR 200 based on area).
