Document Checklist

Ensure all physical documents are prepared as per Japan Embassy standards

Required Primary Documents

  • Visa Application Form: Completely filled and signed by the applicant.
  • Covering Letter: Stating travel purpose, travel dates, and daily itinerary.
  • Confirmed Bookings: Confirmed hotel reservations & round-trip flight tickets.
  • Travel Itinerary: Detailed day-by-day sightseeing and stay schedule in Japan.
  • Original Passport: Issued within the last 10 years, minimum 6 months validity after scheduled return date, with at least 2 blank pages.
  • Passport Bio-Data Copy: Clear photocopy of passport bio-data page (First & Last Page).
  • Previous Passports: All previous passports (if any).

Passport Photograph Specifications

  • 2 Recent Photographs: Taken within the last 3 months.
  • Dimensions: 45 mm high × 35 mm wide.
  • Background & Paper: Plain white background, printed on high-quality matte photo paper.
  • Facial Image: Full face, clear features, no borders.

Financial Documents (Strict A4 Paper Format)

All proof of financial status must be original documents on A4 size paper carrying official bank stamp & signature.

A. For Employed Applicants:

  • Personal Bank Statement for the last 6 months (stamped and signed by the bank on A4 paper).
  • Original NOC from Employer on company letterhead.
  • Salary Slips for the last 6 months.
  • Personal Income Tax Return (ITR) for the last 3 years.

B. For Self-Employed / Business Owners:

  • Covering letter printed on official company letterhead.
  • Visiting / Business Card.
  • Company & Personal Bank Statements for the last 6 months (stamped and signed by the bank on A4 paper).
  • Income Tax Returns (ITR) for the last 3 years.
  • Business Registration Proof / GST Certificate.
Please Note:
  • We may require additional documents after reviewing your case in detail.
  • Appointments will be provided as per availability.
