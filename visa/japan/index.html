Frequently Asked Questions — Japan Visa

Quick answers to key questions about processing, fees, and requirements

How long does Japan Visa processing take?

Japan Visa processing typically takes 5 to 10 working days at Embassy of Japan / VFS after application submission / interview.

What is the fee for Japan Visa assistance?

The fee structure includes ₹1,300 Embassy/VFS Fee + ₹1,180 Trippovention Service Charge + ₹2,360 VFS Submission Charge (Total ₹4,840).

What primary documents are needed for Japan visa application?

Primary requirements include visa application form, cover letter, day-by-day travel itinerary, flight/hotel bookings, passport bio page copy, 2 photos (35x45 mm), and original 6-month bank statement stamped & signed on A4 paper.

Can I get doorstep document pickup for Japan visa?

Yes, Trippovention offers physical document pick-up and delivery services across location boundaries (starting from INR 200 based on area).
