Document Checklist

Complete requirements for business delegates & corporate travelers

Corporate & Business Financials (Strictly A4 Original Papers)

  • Invitation Letter: Official business invitation letter from the host company in Poland.
  • Covering Letter: Printed on official corporate letterhead with company stamp and signature.
  • Business Card: Applicant's original visiting card.
  • Company Bank Statement: 6 months original bank statement stamped & signed on A4 paper.
  • Personal Bank Statement: 6 months original personal bank statement stamped & signed on A4 paper.
  • ITR Documents: Income Tax Returns for the last 3 years.
  • Business Registration: GST Certificate / Business Registration Proof.
  • Standard Documents: Application Form, Cover Letter, Flight & Hotel Bookings, Travel Insurance (EUR 30,000), Passport (6+ months validity), 2 photos (35–45 mm width).

Frequently Asked Questions — Poland Business Visa

Quick answers to key questions about processing, fees, and requirements

How long does Poland Business Visa processing take?

Poland Business Visa processing typically takes 10 to 15 working days upon VFS document deposit after application submission / interview.

What is the fee for Poland Business Visa assistance?

The fee structure includes VFS appointment charge, Embassy visa fee (approx. €90 / ₹9,500-10,200), and ₹5,000 Trippovention file preparation service charge.

What primary documents are needed for Poland visa application?

Primary requirements include valid passport (6+ months validity), travel insurance (min €30,000 coverage), flight/hotel vouchers, day-by-day itinerary, 2 photos, and original 6-month bank statement stamped & signed on A4 paper.

Can I get doorstep document pickup for Poland visa?

Yes, Trippovention offers physical document pick-up and delivery services across location boundaries (starting from INR 200 based on area).
