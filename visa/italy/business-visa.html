Document Checklist

Complete physical document requirements for submission

Primary Documents

  • Completed Visa Application Form: Completely filled and signed by the applicant.
  • Personal Covering Letter: Original letter with travel plan. If the applicant is submitting the applications of his/her family, then all applications must include a copy of the covering letter.
  • Flight Reservation: Confirmed round-trip flight booking with passenger names & detailed travel itinerary.
  • Proof of Accommodation: Confirmed hotel bookings or accommodation details covering the entire stay across Italy and the Schengen territory.
  • Travel Insurance: Minimum coverage of 30,000 Euro / 50,000 USD / 50,000 CHF per person. Coverage of all expenses which might arise in connection with repatriation for medical reasons, urgent medical attention and/or emergency hospital treatment. The insurance has to cover the entire stay in Schengen territory.
  • Valid National Passport: Issued within the last 10 years and with at least 6 months validity after the scheduled return, with at least two blank pages. Handwritten passports and passports with observations regarding the front page (bio data) cannot be accepted.
  • Photocopy of Passport: Clear photocopy of the passport (Front & Back).
  • Passport Size Pictures: A recent, passport-size, color photograph with white background not older than 3 months, showing your head and shoulders, with your face covering 70-80% of the image (size 35 – 40 mm in width, not copied or scanned picture). Soft copy is required.
  • Previous Passport/s: If any, bound together with rubber band, not stapled. Photocopy of previous UK, USA and Schengen visa copies.

Financial Documents

  • Personal Bank Statement: Personal bank statement of last 6 months with stamp & signature from bank.
  • Personal ITR: Personal ITR or tax documents of last 3 years.

Category-Specific Additional Requirements

For Self-Employed:

  • Covering letter has to be printed on the letter head.
  • Business card.
  • Company bank statement of last 6 months with stamp & signature from bank.
  • Business Registration Proof.

For Employed:

  • NOC from the company on the letter head.
  • Recognition letter from the company.
  • Salary slips of last six months.

If Sponsored:

  • Sponsorship letter from the company on the letterhead.
  • Updated bank statement of last 6 months (if available).
  • Tax documents of last 3 years of the company.

Invitee Documents (Host Organization in Italy)

  • Company Registration Proof: Official registration proof of the inviting Italian company (e.g., Visura Camerale).
  • Invitation Letter: Formal invitation letter on the host company’s official letterhead.
  • Signatory ID / Status Proof: Passport copy of the authorized person from the invitee company (front and back) or legal status proof.
  • Host Bank Statement: Bank statement of invitee for last 6 months (if available).
  • Company Tax Documents: Tax documents of last 3 years of the invitee company.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to key questions about processing, fees, and requirements

How long does Italy Business Visa processing take for Indian passport holders?

Italy Business Visa processing typically takes 10 to 15 working days after VFS/BLS document submission and biometric enrollment.

What is the total fee for an Italy Business Visa?

For adults, the total payable amount is ₹ 15,592 (Short-term Embassy Visa Fee ₹ 9,940 + Mandatory VFS/BLS Appointment Service Charge ₹ 652 + Trippovention Consultation & File Prep Charges ₹ 5,000).

What are the requirements for the official invitation letter from the Italian company?

The business invitation letter must be issued on official company letterhead by the host organization in Italy, clearly stating the applicant's details, purpose of visit, duration of stay, and who covers travel expenses. It must be accompanied by the Italian company's registration proof (Visura Camerale), a passport copy of the authorized signatory, and host tax/financial documents.

What are the financial proof and bank statement requirements for an Italy business visa?

The Embassy of Italy requires personal bank statements for the last 6 months bearing an authentic bank branch stamp and authorized signature. Personal and company ITR acknowledgments or tax documents for the past 3 years are also required.

What documents are required from the Indian employer sponsoring the business trip?

The Indian employer must provide an official covering / deputation letter on corporate letterhead, an employer NOC, a company recognition letter, salary slips for the last 6 months, 6 months company bank statements, and company tax documents for the last 3 years.

What are the photo specifications for an Italy Business Visa?

Applicants need 2 recent color photographs (under 3 months old) with a plain white background, measuring 35–40 mm in width, with 70–80% face coverage showing full head and shoulders. Scanned or copied photos are not accepted. Soft copy is required.

Can I visit other Schengen countries on an Italy Business Visa?

Yes, an Italy Schengen Business Visa allows travel across all 29 Schengen member states for business purposes, provided Italy is your main destination or first port of entry.

Does Trippovention provide document verification and doorstep pickup for Italy business visa?

Yes, Trippovention provides complete end-to-end document pre-verification, invitation letter compliance check, appointment scheduling, and doorstep physical document pickup and drop-off services across major Indian cities.
