Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to key questions about Schengen and UK visas

Can I travel to all Schengen countries with one visa?

Yes, a uniform Schengen Short-Stay Type C visa allows you to travel freely across all 29 Schengen member states for up to 90 days within any 180-day period.

Which European embassy should I submit my application to?

You must submit your file to the consulate of the country that constitutes the main destination in terms of length or purpose of stay. If visiting multiple countries equally, apply to your first point of entry.

What is the typical processing duration for European visas?

European visa processing typically takes 10 to 15 working days upon document deposit after your biometric appointment at VFS or BLS.
