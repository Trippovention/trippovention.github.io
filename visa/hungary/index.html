Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to key questions about processing, categories, and requirements

How long does Hungary Visa processing take?

Hungary Visa processing typically takes 10 to 15 working days after VFS document submission and biometric enrollment.

What are the different categories of Hungary visas available for Indian travelers?

Trippovention provides complete guidance for Hungary Tourist Visa (leisure travel and sightseeing), Hungary Business Visa (corporate meetings, commercial negotiations, and trade events), and Hungary Visitor Visa (visiting friends or family members legally residing in Hungary).

What primary documents are required for a Hungary Schengen Visa?

Mandatory documents include a valid original passport (valid for at least 3 months beyond the planned stay with at least 2 blank pages), completed visa application form, detailed day-by-day travel itinerary, confirmed return flight reservations, verified hotel vouchers, Schengen travel medical insurance (€30,000 minimum coverage), 2 recent biometric photos (35x45 mm), original 6-month bank statements stamped and signed on format A4 paper, and personal ITR acknowledgments for the past 2 to 3 years.

Is personal presence mandatory at the VFS Global Hungary Visa Application Centre?

Yes, all applicants aged 12 and above must visit the designated VFS Hungary Application Centre in person for biometric capture (fingerprint scanning and digital photography), unless biometric data was captured for a previous Schengen visa within the last 59 months.

Can I travel to other European countries with a Hungary Schengen Visa?

Yes. A Hungary Schengen Visa allows travel across all 29 member states of the Schengen Area, provided Hungary is your primary destination (where you spend the maximum duration) or your initial port of entry into the Schengen zone.

What are the bank statement format requirements for Hungary visa applications?

The Embassy of Hungary requires original bank statements for the last 6 months strictly printed on A4 format paper, bearing the original bank stamp and signature on each page. The statement must demonstrate healthy transaction history and adequate liquid balance to cover travel, accommodation, and daily expenses.

What additional employment documentation is required for employed and self-employed applicants?

Salaried applicants need an employer NOC on company letterhead, last 3 to 6 months salary slips, and salary bank statements. Business owners and self-employed applicants must submit company registration documents (GST certificate or Certificate of Incorporation), company covering letter, company bank statements (last 6 months, stamped/signed), and personal and business ITRs for the last 2 to 3 years.
