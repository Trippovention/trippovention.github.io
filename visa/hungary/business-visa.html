Document Checklist

Complete physical document requirements for business delegates & corporate travelers

Primary Travel Documents

  • Completed Visa Application Form: Fully filled and signed by the applicant.
  • Personal Covering Letter: Original letter with detailed travel plan and itinerary. If the applicant is submitting applications of his/her family or delegation, all applications must include a copy of the covering letter.
  • Flight Reservation: Confirmed round-trip flight booking with passenger details and intra-Schengen transit proof.
  • Proof of Accommodation: Confirmed hotel bookings or corporate accommodation arrangements covering the entire stay across Hungary and other Schengen states.
  • Travel Insurance: Minimum coverage of 30,000 Euro / 50,000 USD / 50,000 CHF per person. Must cover repatriation for medical reasons, urgent medical attention, and emergency hospital treatment across the entire Schengen territory.
  • Valid National Passport: Issued within the last 10 years and with at least 6 months validity after the scheduled return, with at least two blank pages. Handwritten passports and passports with observations regarding the bio-data front page cannot be accepted.
  • Photocopy of Passport: Clear photocopy of the passport (Front & Back pages).
  • Passport Size Pictures: Recent passport-size color photographs with white background (not older than 3 months), showing head and shoulders with face covering 70–80% of the image (size 35–40 mm width, not copied or scanned). Soft copy is required.
  • Previous Passports & Visas: If any, bound together with a rubber band (not stapled), plus photocopies of previous UK, USA, and Schengen visa stickers.

Invitee Documents (Host Organization in Hungary)

  • Invitation Letter: Official business invitation letter on the Hungarian company's letterhead, clearly stating meeting purpose, agenda, dates, and financial responsibilities.
  • Company Registration Proof: Registration proof of the invitee company in Hungary.
  • Invitee Authorized Signatory ID: Passport copy (Front & Back) or legal residence status proof of the authorized person signing the invitation letter.
  • Invitee Bank Statement: Bank statement of the invitee company for the last 6 months (if available / applicable).
  • Invitee Tax Documents: Tax documents of the host company for the last 3 years (if applicable).

Proof of Financial Status (Strictly Original A4 Paper Format)

All financial documents must carry official bank stamp & signature on A4 size paper.

  • Personal Bank Statement: Original personal bank statement for the last 6 months with official bank stamp & signature on format A4 paper.
  • Personal ITR: Personal Income Tax Returns (ITR-V) or tax documents for the last 3 years.

A. For Employed Delegates:

  • Company NOC: Original No Objection Certificate (NOC) from the employer on official company letterhead.
  • Recognition Letter: Formal recognition/deputation letter from the company confirming business travel purpose.
  • Salary Slips: Official salary slips for the last 6 months.

B. For Self-Employed / Business Owners:

  • Covering Letter: Covering letter printed on official company letterhead with company stamp & signature.
  • Business Card: Applicant's original visiting/business card.
  • Company Bank Statement: Company bank statement for the last 6 months with official bank stamp & signature.
  • Business Registration Proof: GST Certificate, Certificate of Incorporation (COI), or Partnership deed.

C. If Trip is Company Sponsored:

  • Sponsorship Letter: Formal corporate sponsorship letter on company letterhead confirming full financial coverage.
  • Company Bank Statement: Updated company bank statements for the last 6 months (if available).
  • Company Tax Documents: Company Income Tax Returns / audited financials for the last 3 years.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to key questions about processing, fees, and requirements

How long does Hungary Business Visa processing take?

Hungary Business Visa processing typically takes 10 to 15 working days after VFS document submission and biometric enrollment.

What is the total fee for a Hungary Business Visa?

The total payable amount for a Hungary Business Visa is ₹ 16,733 (Embassy Visa Fee ₹ 9,600 + VFS Appointment Charge ₹ 2,133 + Trippovention File Prep Charges ₹ 5,000).

What are the requirements for the official invitation letter from Hungary?

The invitation letter must be issued on official company letterhead by the host organization in Hungary, clearly specifying the applicant's name, passport number, purpose and schedule of meetings, duration of stay, and who will bear travel and accommodation expenses. It must be signed by an authorized signatory accompanied by a copy of their passport or Hungarian resident ID and company registration proof.

What are the financial and bank statement requirements for Hungary business visa applicants?

Applicants must provide original personal bank statements for the last 6 months strictly printed on A4 format paper with official bank stamp and signature, personal ITRs for the last 3 years, and 6 months company bank statements with company registration documents.

What documents are required from the Indian employer sponsoring the trip?

The sending employer must furnish a company sponsorship/deputation letter on official letterhead stating the employee's role, trip purpose, and expense coverage commitment, an employer NOC, recognition letter, last 6 months salary slips, 6 months company bank statements (if sponsoring), and company tax documents for the past 3 years.

What are the photo specifications for a Hungary Business Visa?

Applicants need 2 recent color photographs (under 3 months old) with a plain white background, size 35–40 mm in width, with 70–80% face coverage showing full head and shoulders. Scanned or copied photos are not accepted. Soft copy is required.

Can I visit other Schengen countries on a Hungary Business Visa?

Yes, a Hungary Schengen Business Visa allows travel across all 29 Schengen member states for business purposes, provided Hungary is your primary destination or first entry point.

Does Trippovention provide document verification and doorstep pickup for Hungary business visa?

Yes, Trippovention provides complete end-to-end document pre-verification, invitation letter compliance check, appointment scheduling, and doorstep physical document pickup and drop-off services across major Indian cities.
