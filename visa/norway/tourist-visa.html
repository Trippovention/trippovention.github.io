Document Checklist

Complete document requirements for VFS submission

Primary Documents

  • Completed Visa Application Form: Completely filled and signed by the applicant.
  • Personal Covering Letter: Original letter with travel plan. If the applicant is submitting the applications of his/her family, then all applications must include a copy of the covering letter.
  • Flight Reservation: Confirmed round-trip flight booking with passenger names & detailed travel itinerary.
  • Proof of Accommodation: Hotel reservations or confirmed accommodation vouchers covering the entire stay in Norway and across the Schengen territory.
  • Travel Insurance: Minimum coverage of 30,000 Euro / 50,000 USD / 50,000 CHF per person. Coverage of all expenses which might arise in connection with repatriation for medical reasons, urgent medical attention and/or emergency hospital treatment. The insurance has to cover the entire stay in Schengen territory.
  • Valid National Passport: Issued within the last 10 years and with at least 6 months validity after the scheduled return, with at least two blank pages. Handwritten passports and passports with observations regarding the front page (bio data) cannot be accepted.
  • Photocopy of Passport: Clear photocopy of the passport (Front & Back).
  • Passport Size Pictures: A recent, passport-size, color photograph with white background not older than 3 months, showing your head and shoulders, with your face covering 70-80% of the image (size 35 – 40 mm in width, not copied or scanned picture). Soft copy is required.
  • Previous Passport/s: If any, bound together with rubber band, not stapled. Photocopy of previous UK, USA and Schengen visa copies.

Financial Documents

  • Personal Bank Statement: Personal bank statement of last 6 months with stamp & signature from bank.
  • Personal ITR: Personal ITR or tax documents of last 3 years.

Applicant Category Requirements

For Self-Employed:

  • Covering letter has to be printed on the letter head.
  • Business card.
  • Personal & Company bank statement of last 6 months with stamp & signature from bank.
  • Business Registration Proof.

For Employed:

  • NOC from the company on the letter head.
  • Salary slips of last six months.

If Sponsored:

  • Sponsor’s national ID proof like Passport, PAN card or Resident permit.
  • Updated bank statement of last 6 months with bank stamp & signature.
  • Salary slips of last six months.
  • Personal ITR of last 3 years.

If Student:

  • Student ID card copy and school/college NOC (if traveling during term).

For Retired:

  • Retirement Certificate: Retirement certificate / pension approval letter.
  • Pension Statements: Pension statements of the last 6 months.
  • Other Income Proof: Other Income Proof (if available).

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to key questions about Norway tourist visa processing, fees, and requirements

How long does Norway Tourist Visa processing take for Indian citizens?

Norway Tourist Visa processing typically takes 15 to 20 working days after document submission and biometric enrollment at the VFS Global Visa Application Centre.

What is the total fee for a Norway Tourist Visa?

For adults, the total payable amount is approximately ₹ 15,890 (Short-term Embassy Visa Fee € 90 / approx. ₹ 9,900 + Mandatory VFS Appointment Service Charge € 9 / approx. ₹ 990 + Trippovention Consultation & File Prep Charges ₹ 5,000). For children aged 6–12 years, the total is approximately ₹ 10,940.

What are the bank statement and financial proof requirements for a Norway tourist visa?

Applicants must submit original personal bank statements for the last 6 months bearing the bank's official branch stamp and signature, along with personal ITR acknowledgments or tax documents for the last 3 years.

What documents are required if my Norway trip is sponsored?

If sponsored by a family member or third party, submit the sponsor's national ID proof (Passport, PAN card, or Resident permit), updated 6-month bank statement with bank stamp & signature, last 6 months salary slips, and personal ITR for the last 3 years.

What are the photograph specifications for a Norway Tourist Visa?

Applicants need a recent, passport-size, color photograph with a white background not older than 3 months, measuring 35–40 mm in width, with 70–80% face coverage showing head and shoulders. Soft copy is required.

What additional documents are needed for self-employed, students, or retired applicants?

Self-employed applicants need a covering letter on company letterhead, business card, personal & company 6-month bank statements, and business registration proof. Students require a valid Student ID card. Retired applicants require their retirement certificate / pension approval letter, last 6 months pension statements, and any other income proof.

Can I visit other Schengen countries on a Norway Tourist Visa?

Yes, a Norway Schengen Tourist Visa allows travel across all 29 Schengen member states, provided Norway is your main travel destination where you spend the highest number of days or your first port of entry.

Yes, Trippovention provides end-to-end file preparation, itinerary structuring, flight & hotel booking vouchers, travel insurance assistance, VFS appointment booking, and physical document pickup across major Indian cities.
