Norway Visitor Visa Document Checklist

Complete document requirements for VFS submission

Primary Documents

  • Appointment confirmation letter & signed visa application form.
  • Confirmed round-trip flight reservations & hotel vouchers.
  • Travel Insurance with minimum €30,000 / $50,000 coverage.
  • Valid original passport (min 6 months validity) & bio page photocopies.
  • 2 passport size photos (35–45 mm width).

Financial Status (A4 Format Stamped Statements)

  • 6 months bank statement (original stamped and signed by bank).
  • Self-Employed: Company letterhead, business card, 2-3 years ITR, GST certificate.
  • Employed: NOC from employer, 6 months salary slips, 2 years ITR.
  • Invitee/Sponsor: Host invitation letter, resident ID proof, sponsor 6-month bank statement.
