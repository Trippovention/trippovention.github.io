Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to key questions about processing, categories, and requirements

How long does Denmark Visa processing take?

Denmark Visa processing typically takes 15 to 20 working days after VFS document submission and biometric enrollment.

What are the different categories of Denmark visas available for Indian travelers?

Trippovention provides complete guidance for Denmark Tourist Visa (leisure travel and sightseeing), Denmark Business Visa (corporate meetings, commercial negotiations, and trade events), and Denmark Visitor Visa (visiting friends or relatives residing legally in Denmark).

What primary documents are required for a Denmark Schengen Visa?

Mandatory documents include a valid original passport (issued within the last 10 years, valid for at least 6 months post intended departure, with at least 2 blank pages), completed and signed visa application form, personal covering letter detailing travel itinerary, confirmed return flight reservations, verified hotel vouchers, Schengen travel medical insurance (€30,000 / $50,000 minimum coverage), 2 recent biometric photos (35–40 mm width, 70–80% face coverage), original 6-month bank statements stamped and signed on format A4 paper, and personal ITR acknowledgments for the last 3 years.

Is personal presence mandatory at the VFS Global Denmark Visa Application Centre?

Yes, all applicants aged 12 and above must visit the designated VFS Denmark Application Centre in person for biometric enrollment (fingerprint scanning and digital photography), unless biometric data was captured for a previous Schengen visa within the last 59 months.

Can I visit other European countries with a Denmark Schengen Visa?

Yes. A Denmark Schengen Visa grants access to all 29 member countries of the Schengen Area, provided Denmark is your primary destination (where you spend the maximum number of nights) or your first point of entry into the Schengen zone.

What are the financial proof requirements and bank statement format for Denmark?

The Embassy of Denmark requires original personal bank statements covering the last 6 months, strictly printed on format A4 paper and stamped and signed by the bank branch. Statements must show regular transactions and sufficient maintained balance to cover travel, accommodation, and daily expenses.

What additional documents are needed for employed vs self-employed applicants?

Employed applicants must provide an employer NOC on company letterhead, last 6 months salary slips, and 6-month salary account bank statement. Self-employed applicants must submit a covering letter on company letterhead, company registration certificate (GST certificate or COI), company business card, 6-month personal & company bank statements (stamped and signed), and personal and company ITRs for the past 3 assessment years.
