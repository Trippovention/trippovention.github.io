Document Checklist

Complete physical document requirements for VFS submission

Primary Documents

  • Completed Visa Application Form: Completely filled and signed by the applicant.
  • Personal Covering Letter: Original letter with travel plan. If the applicant is submitting the applications of his/her family, then all applications must include a copy of the covering letter.
  • Flight Reservation: Confirmed round-trip flight booking with passenger names & detailed travel itinerary.
  • Proof of Accommodation: Confirmed hotel bookings or accommodation vouchers covering the entire stay across Denmark and the Schengen territory.
  • Travel Insurance: Minimum coverage of 30,000 Euro / 50,000 USD / 50,000 CHF per person. Coverage of all expenses which might arise in connection with repatriation for medical reasons, urgent medical attention and/or emergency hospital treatment. The insurance has to cover the entire stay in Schengen territory.
  • Valid National Passport: Issued within the last 10 years and with at least 6 months validity after the scheduled return, with at least two blank pages. Handwritten passports and passports with observations regarding the front page (bio data) cannot be accepted.
  • Photocopy of Passport: Clear photocopy of the passport (Front & Back).
  • Passport Size Pictures: A recent, passport-size, color photograph with white background not older than 3 months, showing your head and shoulders, with your face covering 70-80% of the image (size 35 – 40 mm in width, not copied or scanned picture). Soft copy is required.
  • Previous Passport/s: If any, bound together with rubber band, not stapled. Photocopy of previous UK, USA and Schengen visa copies.

Financial Documents

  • Personal Bank Statement: Personal bank statement of last 6 months with stamp & signature from bank (strictly on original format A4 paper).
  • Income Tax Returns: Personal ITR or tax documents of last 3 years.

Category-Specific Additional Requirements

For Self-Employed:

  • Covering letter has to be printed on the letter head.
  • Business card.
  • Personal & Company bank statement of last 6 months with stamp & signature from bank.
  • Business Registration Proof.

For Employed:

  • NOC from the company on the letter head.
  • Salary slips of last six months.

If Sponsored:

  • Sponsor’s national ID proof like Passport, PAN card or Resident permit.
  • Updated bank statement of last 6 months.
  • Salary slips of last six months.
  • Personal ITR of last 3 years.

If Student:

  • Student Id card.

For Retired:

  • Retirement Certificate: Retirement certificate / pension approval letter.
  • Pension Statements: Pension statements (last 6 months).
  • Other Income Proof: Other Income Proof (if available).

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to key questions about processing, fees, and requirements

How long does Denmark Tourist Visa processing take for Indian passport holders?

Denmark Tourist Visa processing typically takes 15 to 20 working days after VFS document submission and biometric enrollment.

What is the total fee for a Denmark Tourist Visa?

For adults, the total payable amount is ₹ 16,990 (Embassy Visa Fee ₹ 9,900 + Mandatory VFS Appointment Charge ₹ 2,090 + Trippovention Consultation & File Prep Charges ₹ 5,000). For children aged 6–12 years, the total fee is ₹ 11,990.

What are the mandatory photograph specifications for a Denmark Tourist Visa?

Applicants must submit 2 recent passport-size color photographs (under 3 months old) with a solid white background, measuring 35–40 mm in width. The face must cover 70–80% of the picture, showing full head and shoulders. Scanned or copied images are not accepted. Soft copy is also required.

What are the bank statement format requirements for Denmark visa applications?

The Embassy of Denmark strictly requires original bank statements for the last 6 months printed on format A4 paper, bearing an authentic bank branch stamp and authorized signature. Personal ITR acknowledgments for the past 3 years are also mandatory.

Can family members travel together under one covering letter?

Yes. If submitting applications for family members together, one primary original covering letter detailing the complete travel itinerary must be prepared, and each family member's application dossier must contain a photocopy of this covering letter.

What documents are needed if my Denmark tourist trip is sponsored?

If sponsored, you must submit the sponsor’s national ID proof (Passport, PAN card, or Resident permit), updated bank statements for the last 6 months, salary slips for the last 6 months, and personal ITR acknowledgments for the last 3 years.

What are the documentation requirements for students and retired applicants?

Students must provide a valid Student ID card and formal leave / NOC letter from their educational institution. Retired applicants must submit their official retirement certificate / pension approval letter, 6 months pension credit bank statements, and other income proofs if applicable.
