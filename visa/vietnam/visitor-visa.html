Document Checklist

Complete document requirements for visiting friends or family in Vietnam

Mandatory Applicant Documents

  • Valid Passport: Scanned copy of passport bio pages (front & back, minimum 6 months validity from travel date).
  • Passport Photo: 1 recent HD soft copy photograph (white background, 4x6 cm, no spectacles).
  • Flight & Accommodation: Confirmed round-trip flight itinerary and hotel/residence address proof in Vietnam.
  • Personal Covering Letter: Stating travel purpose, visit dates, and relationship with host in Vietnam.

Host Documents in Vietnam

  • Host Invitation Letter: Signed invitation letter from resident host detailing visit purpose and duration.
  • Host ID & Status Proof: Copy of host's passport bio page and valid Vietnamese work permit / residence visa.
  • Host Address Proof: Tenancy agreement or residential utility bill in Vietnam.

Important Application Notes

  • Submit clear, high-resolution colour scans of all applicant & host documents.
  • Estimated processing window: 5 to 10 working days.
  • Visa approval is strictly subject to the Vietnam Immigration Department.
