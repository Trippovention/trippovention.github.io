Frequently Asked Questions — Vietnam Visa

Quick answers to key questions about processing, e-Visa eligibility, photo rules, and fees

How long does Vietnam e-Visa processing take?

Vietnam e-Visa processing for Indian passport holders typically takes 5 to 10 working days after application submission.

Do Indian citizens need a visa to visit Vietnam?

Yes, Indian passport holders require a valid visa to enter Vietnam. The 100% online e-Visa is the simplest option for tourism, short business trips, or visiting family.

What is the total fee for Vietnam Visa assistance?

Vietnam e-Visa has 2 major categories: Single Entry (25 USD approx ₹ 2,100 + ₹ 1,180 service fee = Total ₹ 3,280) and Multi Entry (50 USD approx ₹ 4,200 + ₹ 1,180 service fee = Total ₹ 5,380). Urgent processing is available on request.

Can I get urgent or emergency processing for Vietnam Visa?

Yes, Trippovention offers urgent and fast-track processing assistance for travellers needing their Vietnam e-Visa on priority (within 24 to 72 hours). Contact our team via WhatsApp or phone for immediate coordination.

What documents are required for a Vietnam Tourist e-Visa?

Requirements include a scanned copy of your passport bio pages (front & back, min 6 months validity), 1 recent HD photo soft copy (white background, 4x6 cm), confirmed flight ticket, and hotel booking voucher.

Is physical passport submission or embassy visit required?

No, the Vietnam e-Visa is 100% electronic. You do not need to submit your physical passport or visit the embassy. The approved e-Visa document is delivered digitally via email.

What photo specifications are required for Vietnam e-Visa?

The photograph must be a clear HD soft copy with a plain white background, taken within the last 3 months, showing full frontal face view without eyeglasses or headwear.

What is the validity and stay duration of a Vietnam e-Visa?

Vietnam e-Visas are available for single-entry or multiple-entry with validity options up to 30 days or 90 days, allowing entry through designated international airports, seaports, and land borders.

Can I apply for a Vietnam e-Visa for business or family visits?

Yes, Vietnam offers electronic visas for tourist, business, and visitor purposes. Business applicants provide corporate invitation details, while visitor applicants submit host resident details.
