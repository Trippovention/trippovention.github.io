Document Checklist

Complete document requirements for application filing

Mandatory Basic Documents

  • Valid Passport: Scanned copy of passport bio pages (front & back, minimum 6 months validity from entry date).
  • Passport Photo: 1 recent HD passport-size photo soft copy (white background, 4x6 cm, no spectacles).
  • Flight Itinerary: Confirmed round-trip flight booking / flight travel schedule.
  • Hotel Reservation: Confirmed hotel booking vouchers or place of stay address in Vietnam.
  • Online Application Form: Completed e-Visa portal entry details.

Photo Specifications

  • Dimensions: HD digital soft copy (4x6 cm standard ratio).
  • Background: Plain white background, taken within the last 3 months.
  • Facial View: Frontal view, neutral facial expression, full face visible without eyeglasses or headwear (except religious grounds).

Financial & Travel Proofs

  • Hassle-Free Filing: Simple online submission without mandatory bank visit or physical statement stamping for standard e-Visas.
  • Travel Credentials: Valid passport scan, photograph, flight itinerary, and accommodation booking voucher.

Important Application Notes

  • 100% electronic visa process — no physical passport submission or embassy visit required.
  • Turnaround time: 5 to 10 working days.
  • Visa approval is strictly subject to the decision of the Vietnam Immigration Department.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to key questions about processing, e-Visa eligibility, photo rules, and fees

How long does Vietnam Tourist e-Visa processing take?

Processing typically takes 5 to 10 working days after submitting your online application.

Do Indian citizens need a visa to visit Vietnam?

Yes, Indian passport holders require a valid visa to enter Vietnam. The 100% online e-Visa is the simplest option for tourism, short business trips, or visiting family.

What is the fee for Vietnam Tourist Visa assistance?

Vietnam e-Visa has 2 major categories: Single Entry (25 USD approx ₹ 2,100 + ₹ 1,180 service fee = Total ₹ 3,280) and Multi Entry (50 USD approx ₹ 4,200 + ₹ 1,180 service fee = Total ₹ 5,380). Urgent processing is available on request.

Can I get urgent or emergency processing for Vietnam Visa?

Yes, Trippovention offers urgent and fast-track processing assistance for travellers needing their Vietnam e-Visa on priority (within 24 to 72 hours). Contact our team via WhatsApp or phone for immediate coordination.

What documents are required for a Vietnam Tourist e-Visa?

Requirements include a scanned copy of your passport bio pages (front & back, min 6 months validity), 1 recent HD photo soft copy (white background, 4x6 cm), confirmed flight ticket, and hotel booking voucher.

Do I need to submit physical passport for Vietnam Visa?

No, Vietnam issues electronic e-Visas. You only need to upload clear scanned copies of your passport bio page and photograph.

What photo specifications are required for Vietnam e-Visa?

The photograph must be a clear HD soft copy with a plain white background, taken within the last 3 months, showing full frontal face view without eyeglasses or headwear.

What is the validity and stay duration of a Vietnam e-Visa?

Vietnam e-Visas are available for single-entry or multiple-entry with validity options up to 30 days or 90 days, allowing entry through designated international airports, seaports, and land borders.

Can I extend my stay in Vietnam on a Tourist e-Visa?

e-Visa extensions depend on current Vietnamese immigration policy guidelines. Travelers wishing to stay longer should apply for a 90-day multiple-entry e-Visa prior to departure.
