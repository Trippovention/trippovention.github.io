Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to key questions about Australian & New Zealand visas

Is the Australian Subclass 600 visa an electronic visa?

Yes, the Australian Visitor Visa (Subclass 600) is granted digitally and linked electronically to your passport. However, Indian applicants must complete in-person biometrics collection at a VFS Global centre.

Do Indian passport holders need a visa to enter Fiji?

Indian nationals do not require a pre-arrival visa for holiday visits to Fiji for stays up to 4 months. A 4-month visitor permit is granted on arrival upon presenting valid return tickets and proof of funds.

How long does New Zealand visitor visa processing take for Indian citizens?

Immigration New Zealand typically processes standard visitor visas within 3 to 5 weeks. Applications require proof of genuine temporary stay, funds of at least NZD $1,000 per month, and verified travel itineraries.
