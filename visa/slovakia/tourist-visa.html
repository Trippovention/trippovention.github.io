Document Checklist

Complete physical document requirements according to official Slovak consular guidelines

1. Primary Travel Documents

  • Completed Visa Application Form: Fully filled and signed by the applicant.
  • Personal Covering Letter: Original letter with detailed travel plan. If the applicant is submitting applications for his/her family, all applications must include a copy of the covering letter.
  • Flight Reservation: Confirmed round-trip flight booking showing travel dates and traveler details.
  • Proof of Accommodation: Confirmed hotel bookings, package tour, or advance accommodation payments covering the entire stay across Schengen territory.
  • Travel Insurance: Minimum coverage of €30,000 / $50,000 / 50,000 CHF per person covering repatriation for medical reasons, urgent medical attention, and emergency hospital treatment for the entire stay in Schengen territory.
  • Valid National Passport: Issued within the last 10 years, with at least 6 months validity after the scheduled return, and with at least two blank pages. Handwritten passports and passports with observations regarding the front page (bio data) cannot be accepted.
  • Photocopy of the Passport: Clear photocopy of the passport (Front & Back pages).
  • Passport Size Pictures: Recent passport-size color photograph with white background not older than 3 months, showing head and shoulders with face covering 70–80% of the image (size 35–40 mm in width, not copied or scanned picture). Soft copy is required.
  • Previous Passport(s): If any, bound together with a rubber band (not stapled). Photocopy of previous UK, USA, and Schengen visa copies.

2. Financial Documents

  • Personal Bank Statement: Statement of the last 6 months with official stamp & signature from the bank.
  • Personal Income Tax Returns: Personal ITR or tax documents of the last 3 years.

3. Category-Specific Documents

A. For Employed Applicants:

  • No Objection Certificate (NOC): Official NOC from the employer/company printed on company letterhead.
  • Salary Slips: Official salary slips of the last 6 months.

B. For Self-Employed / Business Owners:

  • Covering Letter: Printed on official business letterhead.
  • Business Card: Visiting card / business card of the applicant.
  • Bank Statements: Personal & Company bank statement of the last 6 months with official stamp & signature from the bank.
  • Business Registration Proof: GST Certificate, MSME/Udyam Registration, Certificate of Incorporation, or Partnership Deed.

C. If Sponsored:

  • Sponsor’s National ID Proof: Copy of Passport, PAN card, or Resident permit.
  • Updated Bank Statement: Sponsor's bank statement of the last 6 months.
  • Salary Slips: Sponsor's salary slips of the last 6 months.
  • Personal ITR: Sponsor's personal ITR or tax documents of the last 3 years.

D. For Students:

  • Student ID Card: Copy of valid Student ID card from school, college, or university.

E. For Retired Applicants:

  • Retirement Certificate: Retirement certificate or pension approval letter.
  • Pension Statements: Pension bank account statements of the last 6 months.
  • Other Income Proof: Other income proof (if available, e.g. fixed deposits, rental agreements).

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to key questions about processing, fees, and requirements

How long does Slovakia Tourist Visa processing take?

Slovakia Tourist Visa processing typically takes 10 to 15 working days upon BLS document deposit after biometrics enrollment and document submission.

What is the total fee for a Slovakia Tourist Visa?

For adults, the total payable amount is ₹ 16,799 (Short-term Embassy Visa Fee ₹ 9,933 + Mandatory BLS Appointment Service Charge ₹ 1,866 + Trippovention Consultation & File Prep Charges ₹ 5,000). For children (6–12 years), the Embassy fee is ₹ 4,967, making the total payable amount ₹ 11,833.

What primary documents are needed for Slovakia tourist visa application?

Primary requirements include a valid national passport (issued within last 10 years, 6+ months validity, min 2 blank pages) with front/back photocopies, completed application form, personal covering letter with travel plan, confirmed round-trip flights, hotel vouchers across Schengen, travel insurance (min €30,000 coverage), 35–40 mm color photographs (soft copy required), previous passports bound with a rubber band, 6-month stamped bank statements, and 3 years ITR.

What are the bank statement and financial requirements?

Applicants must provide personal bank statements for the last 6 months with official bank stamp and signature, along with personal Income Tax Return (ITR) acknowledgments or tax documents of the last 3 years.

What additional documents are needed for self-employed or employed applicants?

Employed applicants must provide an official NOC on company letterhead and salary slips of the last 6 months. Self-employed applicants must submit a covering letter on company letterhead, business card, 6-month personal & company bank statements with stamp & signature, and business registration proof (GST/MSME/Incorporation).

What documents are required if my trip is sponsored, or if I am a student or retired?

If sponsored: sponsor’s ID proof (Passport/PAN/Resident permit), 6-month bank statement, 6 months salary slips, and 3 years ITR. If student: copy of student ID card. If retired: retirement certificate / pension approval letter, 6-month pension statements, and other income proof.

What are the photograph specifications for Slovakia Schengen visa?

A recent, passport-size color photograph with a white background not older than 3 months, showing head and shoulders with face covering 70–80% of the image (size 35–40 mm in width, not copied or scanned picture). A soft copy is required.

Does Trippovention provide document verification and doorstep pickup?

Yes, Trippovention provides complete end-to-end file preparation, appointment scheduling at BLS, checklist compliance verification, and doorstep physical document pickup across major Indian cities.
