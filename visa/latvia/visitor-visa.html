Latvia Visitor Visa Document Checklist

Complete requirements for visiting family or friends

Applicant Documents

  • Application Form: Filled and signed by applicant.
  • Covering Letter & Itinerary: Detailing visit dates, relationship, and travel plan.
  • Flight & Accommodation: Confirmed air tickets & hotel/stay vouchers.
  • Travel Insurance: EUR 30,000 minimum medical coverage.
  • Passport & Photos: Min 6 months validity after return date, 2 photos (35–45 mm width).
  • Financial Proof (A4 Original): 6 months bank statement stamped & signed on A4 paper, 3 years ITR, NOC / Salary slips.

Host / Invitee Documents

  • Invitation Letter: Official letter from host in Latvia.
  • Host ID / Status: Copy of host passport, resident permit, or FIN card.

Frequently Asked Questions — Latvia Visitor Visa

Quick answers to key questions about processing, fees, and requirements

How long does Latvia Visitor Visa processing take?

Latvia Visitor Visa processing typically takes 10 to 15 working days upon VFS document deposit after application submission / interview.

What is the fee for Latvia Visitor Visa assistance?

The fee structure includes VFS appointment charge, Embassy visa fee (approx. €90 / ₹9,500-10,200), and ₹5,000 Trippovention file preparation service charge.

What primary documents are needed for Latvia visa application?

Primary requirements include valid passport (6+ months validity), travel insurance (min €30,000 coverage), flight/hotel vouchers, day-by-day itinerary, 2 photos, and original 6-month bank statement stamped & signed on A4 paper.

Can I get doorstep document pickup for Latvia visa?

Yes, Trippovention offers physical document pick-up and delivery services across location boundaries (starting from INR 200 based on area).
