Frequently Asked Questions — UAE / Dubai Visa

Quick answers to key questions about processing, fees, and requirements

How long does UAE / Dubai Visa processing take?

UAE / Dubai Visa processing typically takes 4 to 10 working days after application and document submission.

What primary documents are needed for UAE / Dubai visa application?

Primary requirements include scanned passport cover page (front & back), bio page (6+ months validity), white background digital photo, PAN Card, flight ticket, hotel voucher, and birth certificate for minors under 18.

What entry options are available for UAE / Dubai Visas?

Options include 30-day and 60-day e-Visas in both Single Entry and Multiple Entry options for adult and child travellers, ideal for tourism, family visits, and business travel.

What documents are required for minors under 18 traveling to UAE?

Minors under 18 years require a scanned passport bio page, digital photo, English birth certificate, and copies of parents' passports and valid UAE visas.

Is OK to Board (OTB) approval required for flying to UAE / Dubai?

OK to Board (OTB) is required by certain airlines for specific passport categories. Trippovention assists with updating OTB status directly with airlines prior to travel.

What is the validity period of a UAE e-Visa after issuance?

A UAE Tourist e-Visa is valid for 60 days from the issue date for entering the UAE. The granted stay duration (30 or 60 days) starts from the day of entry.
