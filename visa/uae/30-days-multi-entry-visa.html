Document Checklist

Clear high-resolution scanned copies required (No hard copies needed)

Required Documents

  • Passport Cover Page: External cover page of passport (FRONT & BACK).
  • Passport Bio-Data Page: Clear color scan of passport first and last pages (minimum 6 months validity from travel date).
  • Photograph: Recent passport-sized photograph with plain white background.
  • PAN Card: Clear copy of traveler's PAN Card.
  • Hotel Booking & Flight Tickets: Confirmed round-trip flight ticket and hotel voucher.
  • Birth Certificate: Required for child applicants under 18 years traveling with parents.

UAE Visa Photo Requirements

  • ✅ Photo Size: 2×2 inches (51×51 mm) or 35–40 mm wide
  • ✅ Recent: Taken within the last 6 months
  • ✅ Background: Plain light-colored background
  • ✅ Expression: Neutral face, eyes open, mouth closed
  • ✅ Position: Face centered, looking straight at the camera
  • ✅ Quality: Clear, sharp, high-resolution with good lighting
  • ✅ Glasses: No glare or tinted lenses; eyes must be visible
  • ❌ Avoid: Blurry photos, shadows, dark/light exposure, distracting backgrounds, hats/caps (unless religious), heavy frames, multiple people, or anything covering the face.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to key questions about processing, fees, and requirements

How long does UAE Visa : 30 days : Multi Entry processing take?

UAE Visa : 30 days : Multi Entry processing typically takes 4 to 10 working days after application submission.

What primary documents are needed for UAE Visa : 30 days : Multi Entry?

Primary requirements include scanned passport cover page (front & back), bio page (6+ months validity), white background digital photo, PAN Card, flight ticket, hotel voucher, and birth certificate for minors under 18.

How does the 30 Days Multiple Entry UAE Visa work?

This e-Visa is valid for 60 days from the date of issue. Once you make your first entry into the UAE, you can enter and exit the UAE multiple times within a 30-day window from your first entry date.

Can I use a 30 Days Multiple Entry UAE Visa for cruise trips or side trips to Oman?

Yes! A 30-day multiple-entry UAE e-Visa is ideal for travellers taking Middle East cruises, road trips to Oman, or visiting neighboring countries before returning to Dubai/Abu Dhabi.

What are the photo requirements for a UAE Visa?

Photos must be recent, clear, high-resolution digital images (2×2 inches / 51×51 mm or 35–40 mm wide) with a plain light background, neutral facial expression, looking straight at the camera with no glare or tinted glasses.

What documents are required for child applicants under 18 years?

Child applicants under 18 years traveling with family require a clear scanned passport bio page, digital photo, English birth certificate copy, and copies of parents' valid passports and UAE visas.
