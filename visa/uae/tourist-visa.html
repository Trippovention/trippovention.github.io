UAE Tourist Visa Document Checklist

Clear high-resolution scanned copies required (No hard copies needed)

Required Documents

  • Passport Cover Page: External cover page of passport (FRONT & BACK).
  • Passport Bio-Data Page: Clear color scan of passport first and last pages (minimum 6 months validity from travel date).
  • Photograph: Recent passport-sized photograph with plain white background.
  • PAN Card: Clear copy of traveler's PAN Card.
  • Hotel Booking & Flight Tickets: Confirmed round-trip flight ticket and hotel voucher. (Emirates ID & Tenancy contract / rent agreement if staying with relatives).
  • Birth Certificate: Required for children under 18 years (applicable for transit and multiple-entry visas).

Photo Specifications

  • Format: High resolution digital JPEG photo against a solid white background.
  • Features: Full front face view with eyes open, no shadow, no glasses/glare.
