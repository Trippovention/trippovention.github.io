Business Visa Document Checklist

Required digital copies for corporate travelers

Required Documents

  • Passport Cover Page: External cover page (FRONT & BACK).
  • Passport Bio-Data Page: Clear color scan of passport (min 6 months validity).
  • Passport Photo: Recent photo with white background.
  • PAN Card Copy: Scanned copy of PAN card.
  • Hotel Booking & Flight Tickets: Confirmed hotel & air tickets.
  • Company Covering Letter: Covering letter on company letterhead mentioning trip details.

Frequently Asked Questions — UAE / Dubai Business Visa

Quick answers to key questions about processing, fees, and requirements

How long does UAE / Dubai Business Visa processing take?

UAE / Dubai Business Visa processing typically takes express 24 to 72 hours (1 to 3 working days) after application submission / interview.

What is the fee for UAE / Dubai Business Visa assistance?

The fee structure includes Visa & Embassy Processing Fee starting from ₹7,350 (30-day single entry adult) plus ₹1,180 Trippovention Service Charge.

What primary documents are needed for UAE / Dubai visa application?

Primary requirements include scanned passport cover page (front & back), bio page (6+ months validity), white background digital photo, PAN Card, flight ticket, hotel voucher, and birth certificate for minors under 18.

Can I get doorstep document pickup for UAE / Dubai visa?

Yes, Trippovention offers physical document pick-up and delivery services across location boundaries (starting from INR 200 based on area).
