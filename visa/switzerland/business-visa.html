Business Visa Document Checklist

Complete requirements for business travelers and corporate delegates

Corporate & Host Documents

  • Covering Letter: Printed on official corporate letterhead, signed and stamped by authorized company signatory.
  • Invitation Letter: Official invitation from host company/organization in Switzerland stating trip purpose, duration, and financial coverage.
  • Business Card: Applicant's official company visiting card.
  • Company Financials: Company bank statement for last 6 months (original A4 with bank stamp and signature on every page).
  • Business Registration: GST Certificate, Certificate of Incorporation/Registration, and Company ITR for last 2 years.

Standard Applicant Documents

  • Application Form: Duly filled and signed by the applicant.
  • Valid Passport: Issued within 10 years, 6+ months validity beyond return, 2+ blank pages (No handwritten/observed passports).
  • Photographs: 2 recent photos (35–40 mm width, white background, 70–80% face coverage).
  • Personal Financials: Personal bank statement (last 6 months original A4 stamped), Salary slips (6 months), Personal ITR (2 years).
  • Travel & Accommodation: Confirmed round-trip flight bookings and Swiss hotel reservations.
  • Mandatory Travel Insurance: Minimum coverage of €30,000 / $50,000 USD / CHF 50,000 for medical emergency & repatriation.
