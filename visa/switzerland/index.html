Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Switzerland Schengen visa assistance with Trippovention

How long does Switzerland visa processing take from India?

Embassy processing for a Switzerland Schengen visa typically takes 15 to 20 working days after submission at VFS. We require at least 1 working day to review and compile your document file prior to appointment submission.

What travel insurance is required for a Switzerland Schengen visa?

Travel insurance must have a minimum coverage of €30,000 (or USD 50,000 / CHF 50,000) per person. It must cover medical repatriation, urgent medical attention, and emergency hospital treatment for the full duration of stay in the Schengen territory.

What financial documents and bank stamps are mandatory for Switzerland visa?

Bank statements for the last 6 months must be provided as original prints in A4 format, carrying the official stamp and signature of the issuing bank on every single page.

Can Trippovention pick up my documents and passport from home?

Yes, doorstep document pick-up and return delivery are available on request. Location-based charges start from a minimum of INR 200.
