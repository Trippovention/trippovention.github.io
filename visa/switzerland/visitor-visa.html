Visitor & Invitee Visa Document Checklist

Detailed requirements when invited by a host or sponsored by family in Switzerland

Invitee / Host Documents (From Switzerland)

  • Invitation Letter: Official invitation letter written and signed by the host in Switzerland.
  • Passport Copy of Host: Clear copy of the host's passport (front bio-data and back address pages).
  • Current Status Document: Valid Swiss Visa copy or Resident Permit (PR) copy of the host.
  • Invitee FIN / National ID Card: Clear copy of the host's FIN ID card or Swiss national identity card.
  • Occupation Proof: Host's employment letter / salary slip / work proof (if available).

Sponsor Documents (If Trip is Sponsored)

  • Sponsor ID Proof: Passport, PAN Card, or Resident Permit copy of the sponsor.
  • Sponsor Financials: Sponsor's bank statement for the last 6 months (original A4 with bank stamp & signature).
  • Sponsor Employment: Salary slips for last 6 months and personal ITR for last 2 years.

Applicant Standard Documents

  • Application Form: Completed visa application form signed by the applicant.
  • Valid Passport: Issued within 10 years, 6+ months validity beyond return date, 2+ blank pages.
  • Photographs: 2 recent color photos (35–40 mm width, white background, 70–80% face coverage).
  • Financial Proof: Personal bank statement for last 6 months (original A4 stamped & signed).
  • Travel Bookings: Round-trip flight reservation (+ intra-Schengen travel proof if applicable).
  • Mandatory Travel Insurance: Minimum coverage of €30,000 / $50,000 USD / CHF 50,000 covering medical emergency & repatriation.
