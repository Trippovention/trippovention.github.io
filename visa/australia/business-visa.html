Document Checklist

Complete document requirements for corporate delegates & business travelers

Mandatory Basic Documents

  • Valid Passport: Minimum 6 months validity from travel date; clear colour scans of bio page and all used pages.
  • Previous Passports: Previous passport(s) if any, with copies of valid/previous visas (UK, USA, Schengen, Canada).
  • Recent Photograph: 1 recent HD passport-size photo soft copy (white background).
  • Personal Covering Letter: Stating purpose of visit, travel dates, meeting schedule, and detailed itinerary.
  • Flight & Accommodation: Confirmed return flight itinerary and hotel/accommodation booking (if applicable).
  • Personal Bank Statement: Personal bank account statement for the last 6 months (stamped by bank).

Business Documents (Indian Employer / Business)

  • Company Covering Letter: Printed on official corporate letterhead with company stamp and authorized signature.
  • Business Invitation Letter: Official business invitation letter received from the Australian host company.
  • Business Registration: Business Registration Certificate or GST Certificate copy.
  • Visiting Card: Applicant's corporate business visiting card.
  • Company Bank Statement: Corporate bank account statement for the last 6 months (stamped by bank).
  • Income Tax Returns: Income Tax Returns (ITR) for the last 2 to 3 years.

Employment Documents (For Salaried Applicants)

  • Employer NOC: Employer's covering letter / No Objection Certificate (NOC) on official letterhead.
  • Salary Slips: Official salary slips for the last 3 to 6 months.
  • Employment ID Card: Applicant's corporate employment ID card (if available).

Documents from the Australian Inviting Company

  • Invitation Letter: Official business invitation letter detailing trip purpose, meeting schedule, and visit duration.
  • Company Registration Details: Australian company registration details (ABN / ACN, if available).
  • Company Contact Details: Full corporate address, official email, and phone contact of Australian host company.
  • Conference / Exhibition Registration: Registration confirmation ticket / badge (if attending expo or trade show).

Important Application Notes

  • Additional supporting documents may be requested based on your profile and business background.
  • Certified official English translations are mandatory for any non-English documents.
  • Estimated processing window: 15 to 30 working days.
  • Visa approval is strictly subject to the Australian Department of Home Affairs.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to key questions about Australian host invitation, business travel, and fees

What documents are required from the Australian inviting company?

The Australian host company must issue an official Business Invitation Letter on letterhead detailing the purpose of visit, meeting schedule, visit duration, company ABN/ACN registration details, and full contact information.

How long does Australia Business Visa processing take?

Processing typically takes 15 to 30 working days after ImmiAccount filing and biometrics submission.

What is the fee for Australia Business Visa assistance?

The total fee is ₹ 23,750 per applicant (250 AUD (approx ₹ 13,750) Department Fee + ₹ 10,000 Trippovention Consultation Charges).

Can I work in Australia on a Business Visitor Visa?

No, a Business Visitor Visa (Subclass 600) allows short-term business activities such as meetings, negotiations, site visits, and trade conferences without undertaking paid employment in the Australian labor market.

What is the recommended financial balance for an Australia Business Visa?

A personal or corporate bank account statement for the last 6 months showing sufficient liquid funds (recommended minimum INR 2,00,000+ per delegate) stamped and signed by the bank.

Can a company sponsor multiple delegates on one business visa application?

Each delegate must submit an individual visa application via ImmiAccount; however, the Australian host company can issue a single collective invitation letter listing all attending delegates.

Is biometric enrolment mandatory for Australia Business Visitor Visas?

Yes, after submitting the online application via ImmiAccount, delegates will receive a Biometric Requirement Letter to schedule a biometric collection appointment at VFS.

What activities are allowed under an Australia Business Visitor Visa?

Allowed activities include attending corporate meetings, business negotiations, trade fairs, site visits, and seminars. Taking up local employment or direct sales to the public is prohibited.
