Australia Tourist Visa Document Checklist

Complete document requirements for leisure holidays & sightseeing in Australia

Mandatory Basic Documents

  • Valid Passport: Minimum 6 months validity from travel date; clear colour scans of all used pages including bio page.
  • Previous Passports: Previous passport(s) if any, with copies of valid/previous visas (UK, USA, Schengen, Canada).
  • Recent Photograph: 1 recent HD passport-size photograph soft copy (white background, as per embassy specifications).
  • Application Form: Completed visa application form details (filed online via ImmiAccount).
  • Personal Covering Letter: Detailed covering letter explaining holiday purpose, travel dates, and day-by-day itinerary.
  • Flight & Hotel Bookings: Confirmed return flight itinerary and hotel/accommodation bookings in Australia.

Financial Documents

  • Personal Bank Statement: Personal bank statement for the last 6 months (stamped and signed by bank).
  • Income Tax Returns: Personal Income Tax Returns (ITR) for the last 2 to 3 years (if applicable).

For Salaried Applicants

  • Employer NOC: No Objection Certificate (NOC) on official employer company letterhead.
  • Salary Slips: Official salary slips for the last 3 to 6 months.

For Self-Employed Applicants

  • Business Registration: Business Registration Certificate or GST Certificate.
  • Business Bank Statement: Corporate bank statement for the last 6 months (stamped by bank).

If Visiting Family or Friends

  • Invitation Letter: Official signed invitation letter from host in Australia.
  • Host Documents: Host's passport bio page copy & Australian Visa / Permanent Residency (PR) status proof.
  • Host Address Proof: Tenancy agreement, utility bill, or residential proof in Australia.

If Sponsored

  • Sponsorship Letter: Formal sponsorship letter confirming financial support for travel and stay.
  • Sponsor ID Proof: Sponsor's Passport / PAN / Resident Permit copy.
  • Sponsor Financials: Sponsor's 6 months bank statement and Income Tax Returns / proof of income.

Important Application Notes

  • Additional supporting documents may be requested based on your individual profile and destination plan.
  • Estimated processing window: 15 to 30 working days.
  • Visa approval is strictly subject to the decision of the Australian Department of Home Affairs / Embassy.

Frequently Asked Questions — Australia Tourist Visa

Quick answers to key questions about Australia e-Visa, processing, and financial proofs

How long does Australia Tourist Visa processing take?

Processing typically takes 15 to 30 working days after document submission on ImmiAccount and biometrics enrolment.

What financial proof is required for Australia Tourist Visa?

You need to provide 6 months personal bank statements (stamped by bank) showing sufficient funds for travel, along with ITR for the last 2 to 3 years.

What is the fee for Australia Tourist Visa assistance?

The total fee is ₹ 23,750 per applicant (250 AUD (approx ₹ 13,750) Embassy Fee + ₹ 10,000 Trippovention Consultation Charges).

Is biometrics required for Australia Tourist Visa?

Yes, after submitting your online application via ImmiAccount, you will receive a Biometrics Requirement Letter to schedule a biometric collection appointment at VFS.

What minimum bank balance is recommended for an Australia Tourist Visa?

Applicants are recommended to maintain a personal bank account statement showing a healthy closing balance (approx INR 1,50,000 to INR 2,50,000+ per person) for the last 6 months, stamped and signed by the bank.

Can self-employed or retired individuals apply for an Australia Tourist Visa?

Yes. Self-employed applicants must submit Business Registration/GST certificates and 6-month corporate bank statements. Retired applicants should submit pension statements or retirement certificates along with income proofs.

What is the validity and stay duration of an Australia Tourist Visa (Subclass 600)?

Australia Tourist Visas are usually granted as single or multiple-entry visas valid for 3 months to 1 year, allowing a maximum stay of up to 90 days per visit.

Is travel health insurance compulsory for visiting Australia?

While travel insurance is not strictly mandatory for short tourist stays under 3 months, having comprehensive overseas travel health insurance is highly recommended to protect against emergency medical expenses.
