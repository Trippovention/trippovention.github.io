Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to key questions about African visas

Do Indian travelers need a visa for South Africa?

Yes, Indian passport holders require a visitor visa to enter South Africa, submitted through VFS Global along with 6-month original stamped bank statements and employment proof.

How does the Kenya Electronic Travel Authorisation (eTA) work?

All international travelers must obtain an approved Kenya eTA online before departure. Processing usually takes 2 to 3 business days.

Can Indians get visa on arrival in Seychelles and Mauritius?

Yes! Seychelles grants a Visitor's Permit on arrival (requires pre-arrival Travel Authorization online), and Mauritius offers visa-free entry / permit on arrival for Indian passport holders for up to 90 days.
