Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to key questions about processing, fees, and requirements

How long does Visa Visa processing take?

Visa Visa processing typically takes 10 to 15 working days upon VFS document deposit after application submission / interview.

What is the fee for Visa Visa assistance?

The fee structure includes VFS appointment charge, Embassy visa fee (approx. €90 / ₹9,500-10,200), and ₹5,000 Trippovention file preparation service charge.

What primary documents are needed for Visa visa application?

Primary requirements include valid passport (6+ months validity), travel insurance (min €30,000 coverage), flight/hotel vouchers, day-by-day itinerary, 2 photos, and original 6-month bank statement stamped & signed on A4 paper.

Can I get doorstep document pickup for Visa visa?

Yes, Trippovention offers physical document pick-up and delivery services across location boundaries (starting from INR 200 based on area).
