Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to key questions about processing, fees, and requirements

Which continents and countries does Trippovention provide visa assistance for?

Trippovention provides end-to-end visa assistance categorized across 6 continents: Asia, Europe (including 27 Schengen & UK destinations), Africa, North America, South America, and Oceania.

How long does visa processing take across different continents?

Processing timelines vary depending on the destination country and visa type: Asian electronic travel registrations and e-Visas typically take 1 to 5 business days; European Schengen, UK, USA, Canada, and Australian visas generally take 10 to 15 working days upon document deposit after biometric submission.

What is the fee structure for visa assistance?

To ensure complete transparency and eliminate any confusion between official statutory fees and agency service charges, visa costs are divided into three distinct categories:

1. Sovereign Government / Embassy Visa Fee:

The official statutory fee levied directly by the destination country's government, immigration department, or embassy to process the visa application. Depending on the country, this fee is paid directly on the official government portal (e.g. US CEAC, UK.gov, eVisa portals) or deposited at the visa application centre upon biometric submission. Trippovention does not set, alter, or mark up sovereign government fees.

2. Outsourced Visa Application Centre (VAC) Fee:

Mandatory logistics charges collected by authorized commercial partner centres (such as VFS Global, BLS International, or TLScontact) for biometric enrollment, in-person document intake, counter submission, and appointment management.

3. Trippovention Professional Assistance Charge:

Our transparent consultation and service fee for end-to-end guidance—including expert profile assessment, documentation checklist verification, visa form filing, appointment slot booking, custom covering letter drafting, and submission guidance.

*Note on Country Card Pricing: Cards displaying "Total Est. (Govt. + Assistance)" indicate the overall estimated package cost combining statutory fees and our assistance charges for applicant planning. Where listed separately (e.g. "Govt. Visa Fee + Trippovention fee"), statutory embassy fees and our service charge are shown independently. All embassy and VAC fees are non-refundable once lodged with authorities.

What primary documents are needed for visa applications?

Primary requirements include a valid passport (with 6+ months validity after return date and at least 2 blank pages), recent white-background photographs, travel insurance (minimum €30,000 / $50,000 coverage), confirmed flight/hotel vouchers, day-by-day travel itinerary, 6-month original stamped bank statements, and 3 years ITR.

Can I get doorstep document pickup for my visa application?

Yes, Trippovention offers physical document pick-up and delivery services across major Indian cities for maximum convenience.

How does Trippovention assist with Thailand TDAC and digital travel arrival cards?

For destinations requiring electronic entry declarations rather than traditional sticker or e-visas (such as Thailand TDAC, Malaysia MDAC, Seychelles Travel Authorization, and Maldives IMUGA), Trippovention provides end-to-end digital filing, document verification, and submission support at a transparent Trippovention Assistance Fee of ₹ 499 per applicant with 1 business day estimated assistance time.
