Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to key questions about processing, fees, and requirements

How long does France Visa processing take?

France Visa processing typically takes 15 to 20 working days after document submission and biometric appointment at VFS.

What is the total fee for France Visa assistance?

The total fee structure is ₹ 17,100 for adults (₹ 9,900 Embassy Fee + ₹ 2,200 Mandatory VFS Appointment Fee + ₹ 5,000 Trippovention Service Charge) and ₹ 12,100 for children aged 6–12.

What primary documents are required for France Visa?

Primary requirements include signed France visa application form, personal covering letter, confirmed flight reservation, hotel proof, Schengen travel insurance (€30,000 coverage), valid passport (6+ months validity), 2 passport-size photographs, stamped 6-month bank statements, and 3 years ITR.

Do I need to visit VFS Global in person for biometrics?

Yes, all applicants (aged 12 and above) must appear in person at the VFS Global France Visa Application Centre to provide fingerprints and biometric photograph, unless biometrics were captured for a Schengen visa within the past 59 months.

Can I visit other European countries with a France Schengen Visa?

Yes, a short-stay Schengen visa issued by France allows travel across all 29 Schengen member countries, provided France is your main destination (maximum stay) or first port of entry into the Schengen zone.
