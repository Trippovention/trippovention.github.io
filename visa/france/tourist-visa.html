Document Checklist

Complete requirements for Indian citizens applying for France Tourist Visa

Primary Documents

  • Completed Visa Application Form: Completed and signed France visa application form.
  • Personal Covering Letter: Original letter with travel plan. If the applicant is submitting the applications of his/her family, then all applications must include a copy of the covering letter.
  • Flight Reservation: Confirmed round-trip flight booking or travel itinerary.
  • Proof of Accommodation: Hotel booking confirmation or stay arrangements proof.
  • Travel Insurance: Minimum coverage of 30,000 Euro / 50,000 USD / 50,000 CHF per person. Coverage of all expenses which might arise in connection with repatriation for medical reasons, urgent medical attention and/or emergency hospital treatment. The insurance has to cover the entire stay in Schengen territory.
  • Valid National Passport: Issued within the last 10 years and with at least 6 months validity after the scheduled return, with at least two blank pages. (Handwritten passports and passports with observations regarding the front page / bio data cannot be accepted).
  • Photocopy of Passport: Clear photocopy of the passport (Front & Back bio-data pages).
  • Passport Size Pictures: A recent passport-size colour photograph with white background not older than 3 months, showing your head and shoulders, with your face covering 70–80% of the image (size 35–40 mm in width, not copied or scanned picture). Digital soft copy is required.
  • Previous Passports: If any, bound together with rubber band, not stapled. Photocopy of previous UK, USA, and Schengen visa copies.

Financial Documents

  • Personal Bank Statement: Personal bank statement of last 6 months with stamp & signature from bank.
  • Personal ITR: Personal Income Tax Returns (ITR) or tax documents of the last 3 years.

Category-Specific Additional Requirements

For Self-Employed

  • Covering Letter: Covering letter has to be printed on the company letterhead.
  • Business Card: Corporate visiting / business card.
  • Bank Statements: Personal & Company bank statement of last 6 months with stamp & signature from bank.
  • Business Registration Proof: Business Registration Proof or GST Certificate.

For Employed

  • NOC from Employer: NOC from the company on the official company letterhead.
  • Salary Slips: Salary slips of last six (6) months.

For Retired

  • Retirement Certificate: Retirement certificate / pension approval letter.
  • Pension Statements: Pension statements (last 6 months).
  • Other Income Proof: Other Income Proof (if available).

If Student

  • Student Identification: Student ID card.

If Sponsored

  • Sponsor's ID Proof: Sponsor’s national ID proof like Passport, PAN card, or Resident permit.
  • Sponsor Bank Statement: Updated bank statement of last 6 months.
  • Sponsor Salary Slips: Salary slips of last six months.
  • Sponsor Tax Documents: Personal ITR / tax documents of last 3 years.
