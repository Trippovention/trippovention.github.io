Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about processing, fees, requirements, and document submission

Which High Value & European countries are covered?

We provide comprehensive visa assistance for USA, United Kingdom, Canada, Australia, Japan, Switzerland, France, Germany, Italy, Spain, Netherlands, Austria, Belgium, Bulgaria, Croatia, Czech Republic, Finland, Greece, Hungary, Iceland, Latvia, Luxembourg, Malta, Norway, Poland, Portugal, Slovakia, Slovenia, Sweden, and Turkey.

What is included in Trippovention's High Value Visa consultation service?

Our end-to-end service includes document verification, online form filling (DS-160, ImmiAccount, IRCC, VFS, BLS, TLS), personal cover letter drafting, day-wise travel itinerary creation, flight & hotel booking vouchers, 6-month A4 bank statement audit, biometrics appointment scheduling, and doorstep physical document pickup & drop-off.

What is the typical processing time for High Value & Schengen Visas?

Schengen visas take 10 to 15 working days, UK & Australia visas take 15 to 25 working days, Canada visas take 25 to 40 working days, and USA visas depend on interview appointment slot availability at the embassy/consulate.

How far in advance should I apply for a High Value or Schengen Visa?

Schengen visa applications open up to 6 months prior to travel. We strongly recommend initiating your application 45 to 60 days before your intended departure date to ensure ample time for appointment booking and passport processing.

What financial proof is required for High Value Visa applications?

Most embassies require 6 months original bank statements stamped by your bank showing sufficient balance to cover trip expenses, along with last 3 years Income Tax Returns (ITR), salary slips (if employed), or company registration & financial proofs (if self-employed).

Do I need to attend an in-person interview or biometrics appointment?

Yes, USA visas require an in-person consulate interview and biometrics. For UK, Canada, Australia, and Schengen visas, applicants must visit an authorized Visa Application Centre (VFS, TLScontact, or BLS) to provide fingerprints and a digital photograph.

Does Trippovention guarantee visa approval?

No agency can guarantee visa approval. Visa decisions rest solely with the respective Embassy, High Commission, or Consulate. Trippovention ensures your application file is 100% compliant, properly structured, and error-free to maximize approval probability.

How does doorstep document pickup and delivery work?

After reviewing your online documents, our secure logistics team collects physical passports and required documents from your home/office in major Indian cities and delivers stamped passports safely back to your doorstep post-processing.
