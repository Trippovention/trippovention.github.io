Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to key questions about processing, requirements, and categories

How long does Germany Visa processing take for Indian passport holders?

Germany Visa processing typically takes 10 to 15 working days upon VFS document deposit after application submission or consulate review. We recommend applying at least 4 to 6 weeks before your scheduled travel date.

What are the different categories of Germany Schengen Visas available?

Trippovention provides end-to-end guidance for Germany Tourist Visa (leisure, sightseeing, and vacations), Germany Business Visa (corporate meetings, conferences, trade exhibitions like Messe), and Germany Visitor Visa (visiting family members or friends residing in Germany).

What primary documents are needed for Germany visa application?

Primary requirements include a valid national passport (issued within last 10 years, 6+ months validity, 2 blank pages), Schengen travel medical insurance (€30,000 minimum coverage), confirmed round-trip flights, hotel booking confirmations, day-by-day travel itinerary, 2 biometric photos (35x45mm, 80% face coverage), original 6-month bank statement stamped & signed on format A4 paper, and personal ITRs.

What are the strict bank statement and financial proof rules for Germany?

The German Embassy strictly mandates original personal bank statements of the last 6 months printed on format A4 paper, stamped and signed by the issuing bank branch on all pages. Passbook photocopies, unverified net-banking printouts, or non-A4 formats are strictly rejected.

Is personal appearance mandatory at the VFS Germany Visa Application Centre?

Yes, all applicants aged 12 and above must appear in person at their scheduled VFS Germany Appointment Centre for biometric enrollment (fingerprint scanning and digital photograph), unless biometric data was captured for a previous Schengen visa within the past 59 months.

Can I visit other Schengen countries with a Germany Visa?

Yes. A Germany Schengen Visa allows unrestricted travel across all 29 Schengen member states, provided Germany is your primary destination (where you spend the highest number of days) or your main point of entry into the Schengen area.

What additional documents are needed for employed versus self-employed applicants?

Employed applicants must provide an employer NOC on company letterhead, last 3 to 6 months salary slips, and 6-month stamped salary account bank statement. Self-employed applicants must submit a cover letter on company letterhead, business registration proof (GST certificate or COI), 6 months company bank statement (stamped and signed), and personal and company ITRs for the last 3 years.

What is a Formal Obligation (Verpflichtungserklärung) for a Germany Visitor Visa?

If visiting relatives or friends in Germany who are sponsoring your stay, an official declaration of commitment (Verpflichtungserklärung) obtained by your host from the local German immigration office (Ausländerbehörde) acts as verified legal sponsorship proof.
