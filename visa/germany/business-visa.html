Document Checklist

Complete physical document requirements for corporate delegates & business travelers

Primary Documents

  • Completed Visa Application Form: Completely filled and signed by the applicant.
  • Personal Covering Letter: Original letter with travel plan detailing business purpose and agenda. If the applicant is submitting the applications of his/her family or team, then all applications must include a copy of the covering letter.
  • Flight Reservation: Confirmed round-trip flight booking with passenger names & detailed travel itinerary.
  • Proof of Accommodation: Confirmed hotel bookings or accommodation vouchers covering the entire stay in Germany and Schengen territory.
  • Travel Insurance: Minimum coverage of 30,000 Euro / 50,000 USD / 50,000 CHF per person. Coverage of all expenses which might arise in connection with repatriation for medical reasons, urgent medical attention and/or emergency hospital treatment. The insurance has to cover the entire stay in Schengen territory.
  • Valid National Passport: Issued within the last 10 years and with at least 6 months validity after the scheduled return, with at least two blank pages. Handwritten passports and passports with observations regarding the front page (bio data) cannot be accepted.
  • Photocopy of Passport: Clear photocopy of the passport (Front & Back).
  • Passport Size Pictures: A recent, passport-size, color photograph with white background not older than 3 months, showing your head and shoulders, with your face covering 70-80% of the image (size 35 – 40 mm in width, not copied or scanned picture). Soft copy is required.
  • Previous Passport/s: If any, bound together with rubber band, not stapled. Photocopy of previous UK, USA and Schengen visa copies.

Financial Documents

  • Personal Bank Statement: Personal bank statement of last 6 months with stamp & signature from bank (strictly original format A4 paper).
  • Income Tax Returns: Personal ITR or tax documents of last 3 years.

Category-Specific Additional Requirements

For Self-Employed:

  • Covering letter has to be printed on the letter head.
  • Business card.
  • Company bank statement of last 6 months with stamp & signature from bank.
  • Business Registration Proof.

For Employed:

  • NOC from the company on the letter head.
  • Recognition letter from the company detailing business assignment.
  • Salary slips of last six months.

If Sponsored (Company Sponsorship):

  • Sponsorship letter from the company on the letterhead.
  • Updated bank statement of last 6 months (if available).
  • Tax documents of last 3 years of the company.

Invitee Documents (German Host / Partner Company)

  • Company Registration Proof: German business registration proof (e.g., Handelsregisterauszug extract).
  • Invitation Letter: Official invitation letter on the German company’s letterhead detailing nature of business, duration, itinerary, and responsibility for expenses.
  • Passport Copy / Status Proof: Passport copy of the authorized signatory from the invitee company (Front & Back) or German residence status proof.
  • Bank Statement: Bank statement of invitee company for last 6 months (if available / if host is bearing expenses).
  • Tax Documents: Tax documents of last 3 years of the host company.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to key questions about processing, requirements, and invitation documentation

How long does Germany Business Visa processing take?

Germany Business Visa processing typically takes 10 to 15 working days upon VFS document deposit after application submission or consulate interview. During major trade fair seasons (such as Medica, IFA, or Hannover Messe), appointment slots fill quickly, so we recommend applying 4 to 6 weeks in advance.

What are the mandatory requirements for an official business invitation letter from Germany?

The invitation letter must be issued on official company letterhead by the host company in Germany and signed by an authorized company representative. It must include the applicant's full name, designation, passport details, specific travel dates, agenda of business meetings, and state clearly whether the Indian company or the German host is bearing travel, accommodation, and medical expenses.

What is the fee for Germany Business Visa assistance?

The total fee for an adult applicant is ₹ 17,226, which includes the Embassy visa fee of ₹ 10,100 (approx. €90), mandatory VFS appointment charges of ₹ 2,126, and Trippovention comprehensive file preparation and verification charge of ₹ 5,000.

What documents are required if the business trip is company-sponsored?

If the Indian employer is sponsoring the trip, you must provide a formal sponsorship letter on corporate letterhead confirming financial liability, company registration proof (GST certificate / COI), 6 months updated company bank statement (stamped and signed on A4 paper), and company ITR acknowledgments for the last 3 years.

What are the strict bank statement and financial proof rules for Germany?

The German Embassy strictly mandates original personal and company bank statements for the last 6 months printed on format A4 paper, stamped and signed by the issuing bank on every page. Digital passbook screenshots, net-banking printouts without physical bank seal, or non-A4 format statements are rejected.

Can I travel to other Schengen countries on a Germany Business Visa?

Yes. A Germany Schengen Business Visa allows travel and business meetings across all 29 Schengen member countries, provided Germany is your primary destination where you spend the highest duration of your stay, or your initial point of entry into the Schengen zone.

Is personal appearance mandatory at the VFS Global Application Centre?

Yes, all applicants must appear in person at their scheduled VFS Germany Appointment Centre for biometric enrollment (fingerprint scanning and digital photograph), unless biometric data was captured for a previous Schengen visa within the past 59 months.

Does Trippovention provide document verification and doorstep pickup for Germany visa?

Yes, Trippovention provides end-to-end corporate visa handling, including invitation review, cover letter drafting, flight & hotel itinerary bookings, VFS corporate appointment scheduling, and doorstep physical document pickup and drop-off services across major Indian cities.
