Slovenia Tourist Visa Document Checklist

Complete physical document requirements for VFS submission

Primary Travel Documents

  • Appointment Confirmation Letter: Official appointment confirmation.
  • Visa Application Form: Completely filled and signed by the applicant.
  • Flight Reservation: Round-trip flight tickets with names of all travelers & intra-Schengen proof.
  • Proof of Accommodation: Confirmed hotel bookings, package tour, or advance payments covering stay in Slovenia/Schengen states.
  • Travel Insurance: Minimum coverage of 30,000 Euro / 50,000 USD / 50,000 CHF per person covering repatriation for medical reasons & emergency hospital treatment across the Schengen territory.
  • Valid Passport: Issued within last 10 years, minimum 6 months validity after return date, at least 2 blank pages.
  • Passport Photocopies: Clear color copy of passport bio pages (First & Last page).
  • Previous Passports & Visas: All previous passports & photocopies of prior UK, USA, and Schengen visas.

Photograph Specifications

  • 2 Recent Photos: Taken within last 3 months, size 35–40 mm width, white background.
  • Facial Image: 70-80% face coverage, full face showing head and shoulders, printed on quality photo paper.

Proof of Financial Status (Strictly Original A4 Paper Format)

All financial documents must carry official bank stamp & signature on A4 size paper.

A. For Employed Applicants:

  • Personal Bank Statement for last 6 months (stamped and signed on A4 paper).
  • Original NOC from Employer on company letterhead.
  • Salary Slips for the last 6 months.
  • Personal Income Tax Return (ITR) for the last 2 years.

B. For Self-Employed / Business Owners:

  • Covering Letter printed on official company letterhead.
  • Visiting / Business Card.
  • Company & Personal Bank Statements for last 6 months (stamped & signed on A4 paper).
  • Income Tax Returns (ITR) for the last 3 years.
  • Business Registration Proof / GST Certificate.

C. If Travel is Sponsored or Invited:

  • Invitation Letter & Invitee FIN / ID card copy.
  • Passport copy of host (Front & Back) & status document (Visa / PR copy).
  • Sponsor ID proof, updated 6 months bank statement, salary slips & 3 years ITR.

D. For Students:

  • Copy of Student ID card & Official Introduction letter from School/College/University.
