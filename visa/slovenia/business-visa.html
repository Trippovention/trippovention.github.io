Document Checklist

Complete physical document requirements according to official Slovenian consular guidelines

1. Primary Travel Documents

  • Completed Visa Application Form: Fully filled and signed by the applicant.
  • Personal Covering Letter: Original letter with detailed travel plan and purpose of trip. If the applicant is submitting applications for his/her family or business delegation, all applications must include a copy of the covering letter.
  • Flight Reservation: Confirmed round-trip flight booking showing travel dates and traveler details.
  • Proof of Accommodation: Confirmed hotel bookings or corporate accommodation arrangements covering the entire stay across Schengen territory.
  • Travel Insurance: Minimum coverage of €30,000 / $50,000 / 50,000 CHF per person covering repatriation for medical reasons, urgent medical attention, and emergency hospital treatment for the entire stay in Schengen territory.
  • Valid National Passport: Issued within the last 10 years, with at least 6 months validity after the scheduled return, and with at least two blank pages. Handwritten passports and passports with observations regarding the front page (bio data) cannot be accepted.
  • Photocopy of the Passport: Clear photocopy of the passport (Front & Back pages).
  • Passport Size Pictures: Recent passport-size color photograph with white background not older than 3 months, showing head and shoulders with face covering 70–80% of the image (size 35–40 mm in width, not copied or scanned picture). Soft copy is required.
  • Previous Passport(s): If any, bound together with a rubber band (not stapled). Photocopy of previous UK, USA, and Schengen visa copies.

2. Financial Documents

  • Personal Bank Statement: Personal bank statement of the last 6 months with official stamp & signature from the bank.
  • Personal Income Tax Returns: Personal ITR or tax documents of the last 3 years.

3. Invitee Documents from Host Company in Slovenia

Documents issued by the inviting business partner or organisation in the Republic of Slovenia:

  • Company Registration Proof: Official proof of business registration of the inviting Slovenian company (e.g. AJPES Business Register extract).
  • Invitation Letter: Formal invitation letter on the Slovenian company’s official letterhead stating business purpose, meetings schedule, duration of stay, and expense sponsorship terms.
  • Authorized Person ID: Passport copy of the authorized signatory from the invitee company (Front & back) or legal status proof.
  • Invitee Bank Statement: Bank statement of the invitee company for the last 6 months (if available).
  • Company Tax Documents: Tax documents of the last 3 years of the inviting Slovenian company.

4. Category-Specific Documents

A. For Employed Applicants:

  • No Objection Certificate (NOC): Official NOC from the employer/company on company letterhead.
  • Recognition Letter: Formal recognition letter / deputation letter from the company.
  • Salary Slips: Official salary slips of the last 6 months.

B. For Self-Employed / Business Owners:

  • Covering Letter: Printed on official corporate letterhead.
  • Business Card: Visiting card / business card of the applicant.
  • Company Bank Statement: Company bank statement of the last 6 months with official stamp & signature from the bank.
  • Business Registration Proof: GST Certificate, MSME/Udyam Registration, Certificate of Incorporation, or Partnership Deed.

C. If Sponsored (Corporate Sponsorship):

  • Sponsorship Letter: Sponsorship letter from the sending or host company on official letterhead.
  • Updated Bank Statement: Updated bank statement of the last 6 months of the company (if available).
  • Company Tax Documents: Tax documents of the last 3 years of the company.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to key questions about processing, fees, and requirements

How long does Slovenia Business Visa processing take?

Slovenia Business Visa processing typically takes 10 to 15 working days upon VFS document deposit after biometrics enrollment and document submission.

What is the total fee for a Slovenia Business Visa?

For adults, the total payable amount is ₹ 17,663 (Short-term Embassy Visa Fee ₹ 9,910 + Mandatory VFS Appointment Service Charge ₹ 2,753 + Trippovention Consultation & File Prep Charges ₹ 5,000).

What documents are required from the host company in Slovenia?

The host company in Slovenia must provide Company Registration Proof (e.g. AJPES registration extract), an Invitation Letter on company letterhead, passport copy (front & back) or status proof of the authorized signatory, invitee bank statement for the last 6 months (if available), and tax documents for the last 3 years of the company.

What primary documents are needed from the applicant?

Primary requirements include a valid national passport (issued within last 10 years, 6+ months validity, min 2 blank pages) with front/back photocopies, completed application form, personal covering letter with travel plan, confirmed round-trip flights, proof of accommodation across Schengen, travel insurance (min €30,000 coverage), 35–40 mm color photographs (soft copy required), previous passports bound with a rubber band, 6-month stamped bank statements, and 3 years ITR.

What are the bank statement and financial requirements?

Applicants must provide personal bank statements for the last 6 months with official bank stamp and signature, along with personal Income Tax Return (ITR) acknowledgments or tax documents of the last 3 years.

What documents are required from the employer or for self-employed applicants?

Employed applicants must provide an official NOC on company letterhead, recognition letter from the company, and salary slips of the last 6 months. Self-employed applicants must submit a covering letter on company letterhead, business card, 6-month company bank statement with stamp & signature, and business registration proof.

What are the photograph specifications for a Slovenia Schengen visa?

A recent, passport-size color photograph with a white background not older than 3 months, showing head and shoulders with face covering 70–80% of the image (size 35–40 mm in width, not copied or scanned picture). A soft copy is required.

Yes, Trippovention provides complete end-to-end file preparation, Slovenian invitation verification, appointment scheduling at VFS, checklist compliance verification, and doorstep physical document pickup across major Indian cities.
