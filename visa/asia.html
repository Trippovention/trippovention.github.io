Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to key questions about Asian visa processing

Which Asian countries require visas for Indian passport holders?

Most Asian destinations require either an e-Visa (Singapore, UAE, Vietnam), a sticker visa (Japan), or a pre-arrival digital card / registration (Thailand, Malaysia, Maldives, Sri Lanka, Hong Kong).

How fast are Asian e-Visas processed?

Most Asian e-Visas and digital arrival registrations are processed within 1 to 5 working days depending on the specific immigration authority.

Does Trippovention assist with family and corporate group visas to Asia?

Yes, Trippovention manages individual, family, and corporate group visa applications with complete documentation audit and fast submission.

What is Thailand TDAC Assistance and do Indian travellers need it?

Indian passport holders travelling to Thailand can enter under visa exemption / on-arrival facilities, but must complete the mandatory Thailand Digital Arrival Card (TDAC) prior to immigration clearance. Trippovention provides complete Thailand TDAC application assistance for ₹ 499 per applicant with an estimated turnaround of 1 business day.

What is the Trippovention Assistance Fee for Asian pre-arrival digital cards?

Trippovention offers dedicated online filing and verification assistance for pre-arrival travel registrations across Asia—including Thailand TDAC, Malaysia MDAC, Maldives IMUGA, Sri Lanka ETA, and Hong Kong PAR—at a transparent assistance fee of ₹ 499 per applicant with 1 business day estimated assistance time.
