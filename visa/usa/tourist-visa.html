USA Tourist Visa Document Checklist

Organized by appointment stage to ensure 100% interview readiness

1. Required Documents for OFC / Biometrics Appointment

  • DS-160 Application Confirmation: Printed confirmation page with barcode.
  • Appointment Confirmation: Official US Visa appointment acknowledgment page.
  • Original Passport: Passport with at least 6 months of remaining validity.

2. Required Documents for US Consulate / Interview Appointment

  • DS-160 Confirmation & Appointment Letter: Printed copies.
  • Original Passport: Minimum 6 months validity from departure date.
  • Previous Visas & Passports: Photocopies of bio pages and previous US, Schengen, UK, and Australian visas (if available).
  • Covering Letter: Detailed letter stating travel dates, purpose of visit, and daily itinerary.
  • Flight & Hotel Bookings: Confirmed hotel vouchers and round-trip flight tickets.
  • Financial Proof: Original bank statement of the last 6 months carrying official bank stamp and signature.
  • Income Tax Returns (ITR): Personal ITR for the last 3 years.
  • Accompanying Passenger Proof: Passport and visa copies of co-travelers (if applicable).

Photograph Specifications

  • 2 Recent Photographs: Taken within the last 6 months.
  • Dimensions: 2 x 2 inches (51 x 51 mm) square format.
  • Background & Finish: Matt finish against a plain white background.
  • Facial Image: Head between 1 inch and 1 3/8 inches (22 mm and 35 mm) from bottom of chin to top of head. Neutral face expression.

Category-Specific Additional Documents

A. For Salaried Employees:

  • Original NOC (No Objection Certificate) on company letterhead.
  • Salary Slips for the last 3 to 6 months.
  • Personal Income Tax Return (ITR) for the last 3 years.

B. For Self-Employed / Business Owners:

  • Covering letter printed on official company letterhead with company stamp.
  • Visiting / Business Card.
  • Company & Personal bank statements for the last 6 months (stamped & signed).
  • Personal & Business ITR for the last 3 years.
  • GST Certificate or Business Registration Proof.

C. If Travel is Sponsored:

  • Sponsor's ID Proof (Passport copy / PAN Card / US Resident Permit).
  • Sponsor's updated bank statement for last 6 months (Optional/Recommended).
  • Sponsor's salary slips for last 6 months (Optional/Recommended).
  • Sponsor's personal ITR for last 2 years (Optional/Recommended).
Please Note:
  • Additional documents may be requested after evaluating individual profile details.
  • Visa interview appointments are subject to slot availability at U.S. Consulates in India.
