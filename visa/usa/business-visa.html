Document Checklist

Complete requirements for business delegates & corporate travelers

Primary Documents

  • Completed Visa Application Form: DS-160 Form confirmation page with barcode.
  • Original Passport: Passport with at least six (6) months of remaining validity.
  • Passport Copies & Previous Visas: Photocopy of first and last pages along with previous US, Schengen, UK, and Australian visas (if available).
  • Personal Covering Letter: Original letter with travel plan. If the applicant is submitting the applications of his/her family, then all applications must include a copy of the covering letter.
  • Proof of Accommodation: Hotel booking confirmation or US host accommodation proof.
  • Confirmed Flight Tickets: Confirmed round-trip flight booking or itinerary.
  • Bank Statement: Bank statement of last 6 months with balance of at least INR 3,00,000+ and with stamp and signature of the bank.
  • Personal ITR: Personal Income Tax Returns (ITR) of the last 3 years.

Invitee Company Documents

  • Invitation Letter: Signed invitation letter from host detailing purpose of visit on the company letterhead.
  • Invitee Contact Person Passport Copy: Copy of host's passport bio page.
  • Company Registration Documents: Invitee company registration documents.
  • Invitee Company Financials: Host's 6 months bank statement and tax documents (if available).
  • Occupation Proof: Host's job letter or employment proof (if available).

Category-Specific Additional Requirements

For Self-Employed

  • Covering Letter: Covering letter has to be printed on the letterhead.
  • Business Card: Corporate business card.
  • Bank Statements: Personal & Company bank statement of last 6 months with stamp & signature from bank.
  • ITR: ITR of last 3 years.
  • Business Registration Proof: GST Certificate, Certificate of Incorporation, MSME/Udyam, or Partnership Deed.

For Employed

  • Bank Statement: Bank statement of last 6 months with stamp & signature from bank.
  • NOC from Employer: NOC from the employer on the letterhead.
  • Salary Slips: Salary slips of last six months.
  • Personal ITR: Personal ITR of last 3 years.

For Student

  • Student Identification: Student ID card.

If Sponsored

  • Sponsor Identification: Sponsor Passport / PAN / Resident Permit copy.
  • Sponsor Bank Statement: 6 months bank statement of sponsor (stamped by bank).
  • Sponsor Financials: 6 months salary slips & 3 years ITR of sponsor.
