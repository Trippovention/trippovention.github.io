USA B1 Business Visa Document Checklist

Complete requirements for business delegates & corporate travelers

Invitee & US Host Documents

  • Invitation Letter: Official letter from the business partner in USA (Original or stamped copy, signed, detailing trip dates, purpose, and detailed daily schedule of meetings or training).
  • Host Passport Copy: Front and back bio-data pages of the invitee.
  • Current US Status Document: Valid US Visa or PR / Green Card document copy.
  • Host Bank Statement: Last 6 months bank statement with stamp & signature (if hosting).
  • Address & Occupation Proof: Invitee US address proof and employment proof (if available).

Applicant Corporate & Financial Documents

  • Covering Letter: On Indian company letterhead with company stamp and signature explaining business visit purpose.
  • Company Registration Proof: GST Certificate or Incorporation Certificate.
  • Company Bank Statement: Stamped bank statement for last 6 months.
  • Salary Slips & NOC: Last 3 to 6 months salary slips and employer NOC (if salaried employee).
  • Personal Bank Statement & ITR: Last 6 months stamped personal bank statement and 3 years ITR.
  • Standard Documents: DS-160 confirmation, Appointment letter, Passport (6+ months validity), 2 photos (2x2 inches).

How long does USA Business Visa processing take?

USA Business Visa processing typically takes 7 to 10 working days after US Consulate interview approval after application submission / interview.
