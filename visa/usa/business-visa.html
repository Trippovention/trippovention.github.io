Document Checklist

Complete requirements for business delegates & corporate travelers

Primary Documents

  • Completed Visa Application Form: DS-160 Form confirmation page with barcode.
  • Original Passport: Passport with at least six (6) months of remaining validity.
  • Passport Copies & Previous Visas: Photocopy of first and last pages along with previous US, Schengen, UK, and Australian visas (if available).
  • Personal Covering Letter: Original letter with travel plan. If the applicant is submitting the applications of his/her family, then all applications must include a copy of the covering letter.
  • Proof of Accommodation: Hotel booking confirmation or US host accommodation proof.
  • Confirmed Flight Tickets: Confirmed round-trip flight booking or itinerary.
  • Bank Statement: Bank statement of last 6 months with balance of at least INR 3,00,000+ and with stamp and signature of the bank.
  • Personal ITR: Personal Income Tax Returns (ITR) of the last 3 years.

Invitee Company Documents

  • Invitation Letter: Signed invitation letter from host detailing purpose of visit on the company letterhead.
  • Invitee Contact Person Passport Copy: Copy of host's passport bio page.
  • Company Registration Documents: Invitee company registration documents.
  • Invitee Company Financials: Host's 6 months bank statement and tax documents (if available).
  • Occupation Proof: Host's job letter or employment proof (if available).

Category-Specific Additional Requirements

For Self-Employed

  • Covering Letter: Covering letter has to be printed on the letterhead.
  • Business Card: Corporate business card.
  • Bank Statements: Personal & Company bank statement of last 6 months with stamp & signature from bank.
  • ITR: ITR of last 3 years.
  • Business Registration Proof: GST Certificate, Certificate of Incorporation, MSME/Udyam, or Partnership Deed.

For Employed

  • Bank Statement: Bank statement of last 6 months with stamp & signature from bank.
  • NOC from Employer: NOC from the employer on the letterhead.
  • Salary Slips: Salary slips of last six months.
  • Personal ITR: Personal ITR of last 3 years.

For Student

  • Student Identification: Student ID card.

If Sponsored

  • Sponsor Identification: Sponsor Passport / PAN / Resident Permit copy.
  • Sponsor Bank Statement: 6 months bank statement of sponsor (stamped by bank).
  • Sponsor Financials: 6 months salary slips & 3 years ITR of sponsor.

Important Note

  • We may require additional documents after reviewing your case in detail.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to key questions about processing, fees, requirements, and interviews

How long does USA Business Visa processing take?

USA Business Visa processing typically takes 7 to 10 working days after US Consulate interview approval after application submission / interview.

What is the fee for USA Business Visa assistance?

The fee structure includes ₹18,130 US Embassy MRV Fee + ₹10,000 Trippovention Consultation & File Preparation Charge (Total ₹28,130).

What primary documents are needed for USA visa application?

Primary requirements include DS-160 confirmation, appointment letter, original passport (6+ months validity), confirmed flight/hotel bookings, cover letter, 6 months stamped bank statement, 3 years ITR, NOC/salary slips, and 2x2 inch white background photos.

Can I get doorstep document pickup for USA visa?

Yes, Trippovention offers physical document pick-up and delivery services across location boundaries (starting from INR 200 based on area).

What activities are permitted on a USA B1 Business Visa?

A B1 visa permits attending corporate meetings, industry conferences, trade shows, client consultations, negotiating contracts, settling estates, and short-term company training. Productive local employment on a US company payroll is strictly prohibited and requires an H-1B or L-1 work visa.

What must be included in the US Host Company Invitation Letter?

The invitation letter must be on official US company letterhead, signed by an authorized officer, detailing the applicant's full name, passport number, visit objectives, schedule of meetings, stay arrangements, who covers travel expenses, and a clear clause stating no US salary will be paid.

How early should I apply for a USA Business Visa in India?

Due to consular appointment waiting queues across Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai, Hyderabad, and Kolkata, we recommend applying 2 to 4 months in advance. Trippovention tracks early appointment slot releases daily to help you secure the earliest available interview date.

Can I request an emergency or expedited USA visa appointment?

Yes. The US Embassy allows expedited appointments for urgent, unforeseen corporate travel that cannot be rescheduled (e.g. urgent client technical support or critical contract closings). Trippovention assists in drafting the emergency justification letter and filing the official request on the consular portal.

Can I renew my US Business Visa via Interview Waiver (Dropbox)?

Yes. Indian passport holders renewing an existing B1/B2 visa that expired within the last 48 months (or is still valid) often qualify for the Interview Waiver (Dropbox) process without an in-person consulate interview.

What is the validity of a USA B1 Business Visa for Indian citizens?

Consular officers typically grant a 10-year multiple-entry visa sticker. The duration of stay for each visit is stamped by the CBP officer at the US port of entry (typically up to 6 months per entry).

How does Trippovention prepare applicants for the US Consulate interview?

We conduct comprehensive 1-on-1 mock interview training tailored to your company's profile, meeting itinerary, and financial background. We help you answer questions concisely and organize your supporting folder so you attend your consular interview with total confidence.

What is the difference between USA B1 and B2 visa categories?

The B1 category is strictly for temporary business purposes (meetings, seminars, negotiations), while the B2 category is for tourism, holidays, and visiting family or friends. The US Consulate routinely issues a combined 10-year multiple-entry B1/B2 visa covering both categories.
