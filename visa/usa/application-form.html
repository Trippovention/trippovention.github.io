Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful answers about the questions and details required in this form

What if I don't have confirmed flight tickets or hotel bookings yet?

You do not need confirmed flight tickets or hotel reservations to fill out this form. Enter your intended travel dates and the tentative hotel name or city you plan to visit. The US Embassy advises against purchasing non-refundable tickets prior to visa approval.

What should I provide for 'US Contact Person or Hotel' if I don't know anyone in the US?

If you are visiting for tourism (B2) and have no personal contacts in the United States, provide the name, address, and phone number of the hotel where you plan to stay. For B1 business visitors, mention your inviting business partner, company contact, or conference organizer.

Are my parents' dates of birth mandatory to fill?

No. Father's Date of Birth and Mother's Date of Birth are optional on this form. If you do not know the exact dates, you can leave those fields blank and our visa team will guide you on how this is recorded in your DS-160 application.

How do I enter multiple educational qualifications?

Click the "+ Add More Education" button in Step 7 to add any number of educational entries. You can select Bachelor's, Master's, Diploma, GAD, M.Phil., PhD, Professional Qualifications (like CA, CS, CFA), or choose 'Other' to enter your exact degree or certification.

Which documents are mandatory to upload, and can I share them later via WhatsApp?

You only need to upload your Passport bio-pages (front & back). Income Tax Returns (ITR) and Bank Statements are optional. You can also skip uploading on the web form and share all your documents directly with our visa team via WhatsApp at +91 87508 88875.

What should I select under 'Who will pay for your trip'?

Select 'Self-Funded' if you are paying from your own savings or income. If your employer, company, family member, or a US host is sponsoring your trip, select the relevant option and provide their name and contact details in the sponsor box that appears.

Is my application draft automatically saved if I close my browser?

Yes. All entered information is automatically saved to your browser's local storage in real time. You can safely close your tab and reopen this form on the same device and browser to continue right where you left off.
