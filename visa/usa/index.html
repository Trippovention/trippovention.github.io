Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to key questions about processing, fees, and requirements

How long does USA Visa processing take for Indian passport holders?

USA Visa processing typically takes 7 to 10 working days for passport delivery after approval at the US Consulate interview. Lead times for scheduling OFC biometrics and Consular interview appointments vary based on seasonal demand and consulate location (New Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai, Hyderabad, or Kolkata).

What is the difference between USA B1 and B2 visa categories?

The B1 category is for temporary business activities such as attending corporate meetings, industry conferences, settling estates, or negotiating contracts. The B2 category is for tourism, vacations, visiting family/friends, and medical treatment. Consular officers routinely grant a combined 10-year multiple-entry B1/B2 visa covering both purposes.

What is the total fee for USA Visa assistance with Trippovention?

The fee structure includes the official US Embassy MRV Fee of 185 USD (approx. ₹18,130) + Trippovention Consultation, DS-160 Filling & File Preparation Charge of ₹10,000 (Total ₹28,130) per applicant.

What documents are required for the USA Visa interview?

Mandatory documents include your valid passport (minimum 6 months validity), printed DS-160 confirmation page, appointment confirmation letter, MRV fee receipt, two 2x2 inch recent colour photos, 6 months stamped personal bank statements, 3 years ITR acknowledgement, proof of employment/business, leave approval (NOC), and daily itinerary.

How does Trippovention help with USA visa appointment slot monitoring?

Trippovention provides end-to-end appointment assistance. Our dedicated visa desk assists you through the official US visa scheduling portal, keeps track of early slot openings across Delhi, Mumbai, Hyderabad, Chennai, and Kolkata consulates, and helps reschedule to earlier appointment windows whenever available.

Can I apply for an Interview Waiver (Dropbox) for US Visa renewal?

Yes. Applicants renewing an existing B1/B2 visa that expired within the last 48 months, as well as children under 14 and adults aged 80 and above who meet specific consular criteria, may qualify for an interview waiver (Dropbox submission) without needing an in-person consulate interview.

What happens during the OFC Biometrics and Consular Interview?

The process consists of two appointments: first, the OFC (Visa Application Center) appointment for digital fingerprint biometrics and visa photo capture; second, the US Consular Interview where a visa officer assesses your travel purpose, itinerary, and ties to India. Trippovention conducts comprehensive mock interview sessions to ensure you are fully confident.

Yes, Trippovention provides complete end-to-end document verification, meticulous DS-160 application audits, interview file curation, and doorstep physical document collection and delivery services across major Indian cities (starting from INR 200 based on area).
