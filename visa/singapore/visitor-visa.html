Document Checklist

Complete requirements when visiting relatives or friends hosted in Singapore

Basic Documents

  • Form 14A: Visa Application Form, duly filled and signed by the applicant.
  • Valid Passport: At least 6 months' validity from the date of departure from Singapore.
  • Photographs: 2 recent colour photographs (35 mm × 45 mm): white background, matte finish, taken within last 3 months, one pasted on Form 14A and cross-signed.
  • Cover / Request Letter: Detailing travel dates, itinerary, and purpose of visit.
  • Flight & Hotel Vouchers: Confirmed return air ticket and confirmed hotel booking.
  • Passport Copy: Photocopy of bio-data page and last page of passport.
  • Personal Bank Statement: Bank statement for last 6 months with bank stamp/signature and minimum INR 1,00,000 balance per applicant.

Invitee / Host Documents in Singapore

  • Invitation Letter: Signed invitation letter from the host in Singapore.
  • Invitee Passport Copy: Photo and address pages of the host/invitee's passport.
  • Current Status Proof: Employment Pass, S Pass, PR, or valid visa copy of the host.
  • FIN ID Card: Copy of FIN ID card (front and back).
  • Occupation Proof: Host's employment certificate or occupation proof (if available).

Category-Specific Additional Requirements

A. Self-Employed Applicants

  • Covering Letter: Issued on official company letterhead with stamp & signature.
  • Business Card: Applicant's visiting card.
  • Bank Statements: Company & personal bank statements for the last 6 months.
  • ITR Returns: Income Tax Returns (ITR) for the last 2 years.
  • Business Registration: Company Registration Proof or GST Certificate.

B. Salaried / Employed Applicants

  • Personal Bank Statement: Statement for the last 6 months with bank stamp & signature.
  • NOC Letter: No Objection Certificate (NOC) on company letterhead.
  • Salary Slips: Official salary slips for the last 6 months.
  • Personal ITR: Personal Income Tax Returns for the last 2 years.

C. For Retired Applicants

  • Retirement certificate / pension approval letter
  • Pension statements (last 6 months)
  • Other Income Proof (if available)

D. If Sponsored

  • Sponsor Identification: Sponsor's Passport, PAN card, or Resident Permit copy.
  • Sponsor's Bank Statement: Sponsor's bank statement for the last 6 months.
  • Sponsor's Salary Slips: Salary slips of the sponsor for the last 6 months.
  • Sponsor's ITR: Sponsor's personal Income Tax Returns for the last 2 years.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to key questions about processing, fees, and requirements

What documents are required from the host in Singapore for a Visitor Visa?

The host in Singapore must provide a signed invitation letter, copy of their passport (bio & address pages), proof of current status in Singapore (EP, PR card, or valid visa), copy of FIN ID card (front and back), and employment proof.

What minimum bank balance is required for Singapore Visitor Visa?

Applicants must provide a 6-month personal bank statement with a minimum closing balance of INR 1,00,000 per person. Every page must carry an official bank stamp and signature.

What documents are required for self-employed applicants applying for Singapore Visitor Visa?

Self-employed applicants need a cover letter on company letterhead with official seal & signature, visiting card, 6-month company & personal bank statements, 2 years ITR returns, and Business Registration Certificate or GST copy.

Is the Singapore Visitor Visa single-entry or multiple-entry?

Singapore Visitor Visas issued to Indian passport holders are typically multiple-entry e-Visas, allowing you to visit friends and family in Singapore multiple times during its validity period.
