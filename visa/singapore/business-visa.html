Singapore Business & Invitee Requirements

Detailed documentation depending on your visit category

🏢 Corporate & Business Visitors

For delegates attending meetings, exhibitions, client discussions, or training.

  • Covering Letter: Printed on official corporate letterhead with company stamp and authorized signature.
  • Invitation Letter: Official invitation letter from the host organization/company in Singapore.
  • Business Card: Applicant's official visiting card.
  • Company Financials: Company's last 6 months bank statement (stamped & signed).
  • Tax Proof: Company GST Certificate & last 2 years ITR.
  • Personal Bank Statement: 6 months personal statement with min ₹75,000 balance.
⚡ Fast Turnaround: Our corporate visa desk ensures swift review of company letterheads and invitation formats to eliminate submission delays.

💌 Visiting Family / Personal Invitee

For applicants invited by a Singapore Permanent Resident (PR) or Work Pass holder.

  • Invitation Letter: Formal invitation letter signed by the host in Singapore.
  • Invitee Passport Copy: Clear copy of inviter's passport (first and last pages).
  • Invitee Status Copy: Valid Employment Pass, S Pass, PR Certificate, or valid visa.
  • Invitee FIN Card: Copy of the inviter's FIN ID Card (front and back).
  • Occupation Proof: Inviter's employment letter or company ID (if available).
  • Applicant Bank Statement: 6 months personal statement with min ₹75,000 balance.
📌 Important: Ensure the FIN ID Card and status documents are active for the entire duration of your stay in Singapore.

Standard Applicant Documents

Every applicant must also provide the following standard credentials

Form 14A: Duly filled in block letters & signed.
Passport: Min 6 months validity + copy (front & back).
2 Photographs: 35x45mm, matt finish, white background.
Flight & Hotel: Confirmed round-trip tickets & stay proof.
