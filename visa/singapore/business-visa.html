Business Visa Document Checklist

In addition to standard applicant documents below

Corporate & business visit documents

  • Covering letter on official corporate letterhead with stamp and authorized signature.
  • Invitation letter from the host organization in Singapore.
  • Business card of the applicant.
  • Company bank statement — last 6 months, stamped and signed.
  • GST certificate and company ITR for the last 2 years.
  • Personal bank statement — 6 months with minimum closing balance of INR 1,00,000.

Standard documents (all applicants)

  • Form 14A filled and signed • Passport (6+ months validity) and copies
  • Two photographs (35×45 mm, white background) • Confirmed flights and hotel
  • Cover letter with travel dates and itinerary
