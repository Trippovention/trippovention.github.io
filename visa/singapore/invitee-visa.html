Invitee Visa Document Checklist

Documents when your host is in Singapore

Host / invitee in Singapore

  • Invitation letter signed by the host in Singapore.
  • Invitee passport copy (photo and address pages).
  • Invitee status — Employment Pass, S Pass, PR, or valid visa copy.
  • FIN ID card copy (front and back).
  • Occupation proof for the host (if available).

Applicant documents

  • Form 14A • Passport and copies • Photographs • Flights and hotel
  • Personal bank statement — 6 months, min INR 75,000 closing balance, bank stamp on pages
  • Cover letter with travel dates and purpose of visit
