Required Documents Checklist

Select your employment category to view required documents

📌 General Mandatory Documents (For All Applicants)

1. Application Form 14A: Duly filled and signed by the applicant.
2. Valid Passport: Passport with at least 6 months validity from departure date + front/back copy.
3. Travel Bookings: Confirmed Hotel booking vouchers & Round-trip Air Tickets.
4. Covering Letter: Request / Cover letter stating travel dates and itinerary.
5. Passport Photographs: 2 recent photos as per official Singapore embassy specs (see details below).
6. Personal Bank Statement: Last 6 months personal statement with minimum balance of INR 75,000 per person, with bank official's stamp & signature.

Additional Documents for Employed Applicants

  • Last 6 months Bank Statement with bank official stamp and signature.
  • NOC (No Objection Certificate) from employer on company letterhead.
  • Salary Slips of the last 6 months.
  • Personal Income Tax Return (ITR) of the last 2 years.

Additional Documents for Self-Employed / Business Owners

  • Covering letter printed on official business letterhead.
  • Business Card / Visiting Card.
  • Company & Personal Bank Statements of the last 6 months (stamped & signed).
  • Income Tax Return (ITR) of last 2 years.
  • Business Registration Proof or GST Certificate.

Additional Documents if Invitee is Available in Singapore

  • Formal Invitation Letter from the Singapore inviter.
  • Passport copy of the invitee (Front and back pages).
  • Invitee's Current Status document (Employment Pass, S Pass, PR document, or valid visa copy).
  • Invitee FIN ID Card copy.
  • Invitee Occupation Proof (if available).

Additional Documents for Sponsored Applicants

  • Sponsor's national ID proof (Passport, PAN Card, or Resident Permit).
  • Sponsor's updated bank statement for the last 6 months (stamped & signed).
  • Sponsor's salary slips of the last 6 months.
  • Sponsor's personal ITR of the last 2 years.

📷 Official Photograph Specifications

Size: 35 mm wide by 45 mm high without border, taken within the last 3 months.
Finish & Background: Matt finish against a plain white background.
Facial Image: 25 mm to 35 mm from chin to crown. Full face without headgear (unless religious customs require, but facial features must not be hidden).
One photograph must be pasted on Form 14A and cross-signed by the applicant.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Singapore Tourist Visa for Indians

How long does it take to process a Singapore Tourist Visa?

Standard processing takes between 4 to 10 working days from the date of submission to the Singapore High Commission or authorized visa processing center.

What is the minimum bank balance required for Singapore Visa?

A personal bank statement for the last 6 months with a minimum closing balance of INR 75,000 per applicant (duly stamped and signed by bank official) is mandatory.

Is Singapore Visa single entry or multiple entry?

Singapore tourist visas issued to Indian passport holders are typically multiple-entry visas valid for up to 30 days of stay per visit within the validity period granted by the Embassy.

Does Trippovention pick up physical documents from my home?

Yes, document pick-up and drop-off services are available upon request. Location-based charges start at a minimum of INR 200.
