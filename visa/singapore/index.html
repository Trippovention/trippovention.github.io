Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to key questions about processing, fees, and requirements

Is the Singapore Visa single-entry or multiple-entry?

Singapore Visas issued to Indian passport holders are typically multiple-entry e-Visas, allowing you to enter Singapore multiple times during its validity — ideal for sightseeing, visiting family, and cruise trips.

What minimum bank balance is required for Singapore Visa?

Applicants must provide a 6-month personal bank statement with a minimum closing balance of INR 1,00,000 per person. Every page must carry an official bank stamp and signature.

What primary documents are needed for Singapore visa application?

Primary requirements include valid passport (6+ months validity), flight/hotel vouchers, day-by-day itinerary, 2 photos, and original 6-month bank statement stamped & signed on A4 paper.

Can I get doorstep document pickup for Singapore visa?

Yes, Trippovention offers physical document pick-up and delivery services across location boundaries (starting from INR 200 based on area).
