Singapore Tourist Visa Document Checklist

Ensure all documents are complete before submitting to our visa specialists

1. Primary Travel Documents

  • Application Form 14A: Duly filled in block letters and signed by the applicant.
  • Original Passport: Passport with at least 6 months validity beyond departure date from Singapore.
  • Passport Photocopies: Clear color copy of passport's first (photo page) and last (address page) pages.
  • Cover/Request Letter: Stating travel dates, purpose of visit, and complete day-by-day travel itinerary.
  • Confirmed Travel Tickets: Round-trip air tickets to and from Singapore.
  • Hotel Vouchers: Confirmed hotel bookings covering the entire duration of stay.

2. Passport Photograph Specifications

  • 2 Recent Photographs: Taken within the last 3 months.
  • Dimensions: 35 mm wide by 45 mm high without border.
  • Background & Finish: Matt finish against a plain white background.
  • Facial Image: 25 mm to 35 mm from chin to crown (full face, clear features).
  • One photo pasted on Form 14A and cross-signed by applicant.

3. Financial Proof & Bank Statement

  • Personal Bank Statement: Statement of personal bank account for the last 6 months.
  • Minimum Balance: Minimum closing balance of INR 75,000 per person.
  • Bank Validation: Must carry official stamp and signature from bank authority on every page.

4. Category-Specific Additional Requirements

A. For Salaried Employees:

  • Original NOC (No Objection Certificate) on company letterhead.
  • Salary Slips for the last 6 months.
  • Personal Income Tax Return (ITR) for the last 2 years.

B. For Self-Employed / Business Owners:

  • Covering letter printed on official company letterhead.
  • Visiting / Business Card.
  • Company & Personal bank statements for last 6 months (stamped & signed).
  • Income Tax Return (ITR) for the last 2 years.
  • GST Certificate or Business Registration Proof.

C. If Travel is Sponsored:

  • Sponsor's ID Proof (Passport / PAN Card / Resident Permit).
  • Sponsor's last 6 months stamped bank statement & salary slips.
  • Sponsor's personal ITR for last 2 years.
